    Decay and repair ✓ Live

    Structures lose Durability over time — on a Domain they break after 7 days (repairable) · outside a Domain they vanish after 1 day · on a Tamed Island they never decay · an unused bonfire dies out in 3 minutes and vanishes 1 minute later · repair with Building Repair Kits or instantly with Warp Gems

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Every finished structure has Durability, which keeps dropping over time (even when nobody is around). How fast it drops and what happens at zero depends on where it stands — your own Domain is far safer (Domain).

    #What you can do

    • Check a structure's condition on its info page (Durability / condition)
    • Repair it with Building Repair Kits or Repair Now with Warp Gems → Durability is full again and the countdown restarts
    • Package it (Building) — Durability does not drop while it sits in your bag · when placed again it keeps the Durability it had when packaged
    • Pick up the item bundles left behind by structures that fell apart

    #How fast things decay ✓ Live

    Where it standsDurability runs out inAt zeroNobody uses it
    Any player's Domain (yours, a friend's, anyone's) · clan Enclave — no matter who built it7 daysbreaks and cannot be used until repaired — it does not vanish, contents stayno effect
    Outside any Domain (every island) · Safe House Island1 dayvanishes, contents drop as bundles on the groundunused for 10 minutes → vanishes at once
    Everything on a Tamed Island (the shared Tamed Islands and Supporters' private Tamed Islands)does not decay — Durability stays fixed—no effect
    Player-placed Bonfire · Large Bonfire · Cooking Bonfire (anywhere)1 dayvanishesthe fire dies out in 3 minutes · out for 1 minute → vanishes (below)
    Interior Brazier (anywhere)1 dayvanishesno effect

    Bonfires and Interior Braziers always decay as in the last two rows, even on a Tamed Island.

    • Durability drops in a straight line over that period regardless of its maximum (every structure takes the time in the table)
    • The "nobody uses it" clock starts when construction finishes or when you place a packaged structure (shop items included) · it counts as used when someone actually does something with it, e.g. opens a box, puts in/takes out items, crafts at a workbench, lights a fire, opens a gate, repairs it
    • Unfinished construction sites do not decay or vanish
    • If nobody is nearby, the removal happens when someone next comes close to that area, but the time keeps running the whole while
    • When the owner reduces or cancels a Domain over structures, those structures follow the outside-Domain rule at once (Durability carries on from where it was) — if nobody has used one for over 10 minutes it vanishes almost immediately, so package things first

    ⚠️ Caution

    Sitting or sleeping next to a tent that stands outside a Domain does not count as using it — a tent outside a Domain vanishes 10 minutes after it is finished if nobody does anything with it. Build tents on your own Domain if you want to keep them

    #Unfinished sites outside a Domain ⏳ Not ready

    A construction site that is not finished yet, stands outside your own and your clan's Domain, and has not been touched by anyone for 24 hours is removed automatically — the materials you put in are mailed back · finished structures are not affected (the decay rules above apply to them) · details in Building

    #Bonfires dying out and disappearing ✓ Live

    Player-placed bonfires (Bonfire · Large Bonfire · Cooking Bonfire) are short-lived — on every island, your own Domain included

    StateWhat happens
    Lit, but nobody uses it for 3 minutesthe fire dies out and it becomes an unlit bonfire
    Out (died out · Extinguish Fire pressed · put out by heavy rain/heavy snow) for 1 minutethe bonfire vanishes · anything left on it drops as a bundle on the ground
    • Using it = opening its menu · roasting/crafting · starting unattended crafting · resting next to it while it is lit · lighting it → the 3 minutes start over · anyone's use counts, not just the owner's
    • While it is out, only lighting it before the minute is up saves it (just opening the menu doesn't extend the time) · light it too late and you get a Thai-only message saying it has been out too long and can't be relit
    • An unattended crafting job on it → it neither dies out nor vanishes until the job is done, then the timers continue from that moment — come back for your items
    • An unlit bonfire: no roasting/crafting/starting jobs (Thai-only message «กองไฟดับอยู่ — จุดไฟก่อน», "the bonfire is out — light it first"), but finished jobs can still be collected · you can still sit there, but it is a plain rest: no warming up
    • Resting at an island's own bonfire (e.g. in a camp) with a lit player bonfire within resting range also counts as using that bonfire
    • Not covered by this rule: Interior Brazier (decays in 1 day as in the table) · bonfires placed by the game itself (never die out or vanish)

    #When it breaks on your own Domain ✓ Live

    • Only Repair and Demolish remain in its menu (it can't be packaged)
    • Your own box: its owner can still open it and take items out, but can't store anything until it is repaired
    • Personal Storage, workbenches, pens and everything else can't be used until repaired — contents are safe · nobody else can use it at all
    • It never vanishes by itself; you can repair it any time

    #When it vanishes outside a Domain

    Everything inside — box contents, every storage tab, reins in pens, wall decorations — drops as Abandoned Objects (bundles on the ground) where the structure stood.

    ItemValue
    Items per bundleup to 80 (more items = several bundles)
    Bundle lifetime24 hours
    Who can pick it upanyone (touch the bundle → Pick Up)

    The bundles hold everything: box contents · wall decorations · workbench jobs (finished = the product · unfinished = the materials) · incoming cargo

    #Repairing

    1. Touch a structure whose Durability is not full → Repair (stand within 4 tiles)
    2. Choose Building Repair Kits from your bag whose total repair power ≥ the required repair power
    3. Wait for the 5-second bar → Durability is always restored to full (no failure roll) and decay starts over · the kits are used up

    Anyone can repair a structure; you don't have to be the owner.

    KitRepair power
    Budget Building Repair Kit30
    Standard Building Repair Kit90
    Quality Building Repair Kit150

    Required repair power depends on the structure type and level (minimum 5) — examples:

    StructureLevel 1Level 30Level 50Level 60
    Small Box5103040
    House5204060
    Personal Box5306080
    Personal Storage3080120140

    Energy used to repair = required repair power × 0.8

    💡 Tip

    Repair before Durability runs out — an outside structure repaired in time gets another full day · one on your Domain gets another 7 days

    The Repair option only appears on structures that can be repaired — tents, bonfires and house roofs can't be repaired (no option).

    #Repair Now with Warp Gems ✓ Live

    No repair kit? Press Repair Now in the repair window and pay Warp Gems instead.

    • Cost = the required repair power, in Gems (the same number the confirm window shows) — e.g. a level 30 House (repair power 20) = 20 Gems · level 60 Personal Storage (140) = 140 Gems
    • 5-second bar → full Durability · energy/fatigue are the same as repairing with kits
    • Not enough Gems = no repair (the game says Gems are short and how many you need) · if the repair fails after the Gems are taken, they are refunded (with a message)
    • Warp Gems can be exchanged from Durango Coins in the shop — see Warp Gems

    ℹ️ Note

    Max Durability uses the original-game scale (see Building) — decay times, conditions and the share of materials returned on demolish are unchanged · demolishing a badly decayed structure returns materials based on its real remaining Durability
