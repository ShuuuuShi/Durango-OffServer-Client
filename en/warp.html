    Warps & Rifts ✓ Live

    Warp Holes on an island · quick-warp buttons (Harbor, Waypoint, Domain) · Rifts and the Warp Accelerator (1,000 T-Stone entry, rewards, weekly cap) · Dormant Craters

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    The Warp that shattered Durango into islands left traces you can use — Warp Holes carry you across an island in an instant, quick-warp buttons take you back to the harbor or home, and Unstable Islands have Rifts where you can start a Warp Accelerator: fight off waves of animals pulled in by the Warp for T-Stones, exp and rare materials.

    #What you can do

    • Find Warp Holes on an island and warp between the ones you've found — free
    • Use quick warps To Harbor · To Waypoint · Return to Domain — free
    • Start a Warp Accelerator at a Rift, invite friends, clear each tier's animals in time and collect rewards
    • Open a Dormant Crater so natural resources warp in around it (needs Resource Induction Stones)

    #Warp Holes ✓ Live

    Warp Holes exist on Unstable Islands level 35–60, Civilized Islands, event islands and the Safe House — islands level 15–30, the training island and Tamed Islands have none.

    1. Explore until you find a Warp Hole — the first time you find one you get a discovery reward
    2. You need to find at least 2 Warp Holes on the same island before you can warp ("You need to find at least two Warp Holes to use them.")
    3. Tap a Warp Hole → Warp → pick the destination hole on the map → wait 2 seconds
    • Warping between holes is free · only holes on the island you're on
    • At the Safe House, every Warp Hole counts as found from the start
    • When you die you can revive at a Warp Hole you've found — see Death

    #Rifts & the Warp Accelerator ✓ Live

    A Rift is a crack in the ground leaking Warp energy. There are 4 per island (6 on Volcano islands) on Unstable Islands level 35–60 (except Deep Swamp), the temperate/tropical/tundra Civilized Islands and some event islands — none on level 15–30 islands, Tamed Islands or the Safe House.

    #How to play

    1. Tap an idle Rift → start the Warp Accelerator, paying the 1,000 T-Stone entry fee — everyone on the island gets an announcement
    2. Friends have 10 seconds to tap the Rift and join (also 1,000 T-Stones · within 10 tiles of the Rift · up to 10 players)
    3. Each tier, guard animals (the island's predators) appear around the Rift in 2 waves — clear them all within 60 seconds
    4. After a tier you get a 15-second break, then the next tier starts, up to 10 tiers · from tier 3, Allosaurus join the waves
    5. Run out of time = the run ends · tap the Rift to collect rewards within 2 minutes (once per player) · failing tier 1 = no reward
    6. The Rift then rests for 3 minutes before a new run can start
    • Leaving the island or disconnecting mid-run removes you from the run — no reward for that run
    • If starting/joining fails, your T-Stones are refunded

    #Dormant Craters ✓ Live

    #Tips

    💡 Tip

    When you land on a new island, find at least 2 Warp Holes first — you get discovery T-Stones and a fast escape if an aggressive animal shows up.

    💡 Tip

    The Warp Accelerator pays best on high-level islands with a team that can clear many tiers — at level 35 you need 6 tiers just to earn back the entry fee in T-Stones, while at level 60, 3 tiers already puts you ahead (before counting exp and materials).

    ⚠️ Caution

    Don't leave the island or close the game mid-run — you'll drop out of the run and get no reward, even though you paid the entry fee.
