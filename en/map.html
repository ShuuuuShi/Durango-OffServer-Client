    Map and exploration ✓ Live

    The island map · the Search button for points of interest · discovering warp holes/craters/rifts pays T-Stones · a big reward for finding everything on an island · encyclopedia

    Open the Map to see your island, the points you've discovered and where you are · press Search (the magnifier) to find points of interest around you · discovering a new point pays T-Stones and Survival EXP the first time

    #Points of interest on the map

    PointWhat it isRead more
    HarborSail to other islands · return to a Stable IslandIslands & Travel
    Warp HoleWarp across the islandWarps & Rifts
    Courier Warp HoleSend/receive cargoTransport and Vehicles
    Crater · ground riftSpecial resources · Warp AcceleratorWarps & Rifts
    Faction spotTake missions / deliver items for a factionOrganizations

    #The Search button ✓ Live

    ItemValue
    Energy per search5
    Wait between searches10 seconds (skills shorten it — below)
    ResultThe 3 nearest points of interest

    Not enough energy = no search («หมดแรง — พักก่อนค่อยค้นหา», Thai-only server message) · too soon = «ยังค้นหาไม่ได้ — รอสักครู่»

    #Cooldown by Exploration skill ✓ Live

    SkillWait between searches
    None10 seconds
    Exploration I / II / III9 / 8 / 7 seconds
    • The Search button shows a countdown ring that matches the time the server really uses
    • Pressing it while waiting shows a wait message and costs no energy

    #Discovery rewards ✓ Live

    Walk up to a point of interest you haven't discovered on that island → it stays on your map for good

    DiscoveredT-StonesAlso
    Warp Hole · Courier Warp Hole400Survival EXP by island level
    Crater · ground rift400Survival EXP by island level
    Rift (Warp Accelerator)400Survival EXP by island level
    Harbor · faction spot—Survival EXP by island level
    Every point on the island4,000 (claim once per island)—
    • Only the first discovery of each point counts
    • Claiming the full reward before finding everything = «ยังสำรวจจุดสำคัญไม่ครบ» (not all points found yet)

    #Map vouchers ✓ Live

    Use a map voucher to reveal the whole island map without walking it · vouchers are kept separately in your wallet (Market and Shop)

    #Encyclopedia ✓ Live

    • Farming encyclopedia — each seed has its own level (max 20), +20 EXP per harvest · pick a specialization every 5 levels — details on Farming
    • Discovering a new animal species for the first time gives Survival EXP (Skills and Careers)

    💡 Tip

    Every island pays 400 per point + 4,000 when complete — fully exploring your first islands is a great T-Stone source for new players
