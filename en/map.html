    Map and exploration ✓ Live

    The island map · the Search button for points of interest · discovering warp holes/craters/rifts pays T-Stones · a big reward for finding everything on an island · encyclopedia

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Open the Map to see your island, the points you've discovered and where you are · press Search (the magnifier) to find points of interest around you · discovering a new point pays T-Stones and Survival EXP the first time

    ItemValue
    Energy per search5
    Wait between searches10 seconds (skills shorten it — below)
    ResultThe 3 nearest points of interest

    Not enough energy = no search («หมดแรง — พักก่อนค่อยค้นหา», Thai-only server message) · too soon = «ยังค้นหาไม่ได้ — รอสักครู่»

    SkillWait between searches
    None10 seconds

    • The Search button shows a countdown ring that matches the time the server really uses
    • Pressing it while waiting shows a wait message and costs no energy

    #Discovery rewards ✓ Live

    Walk up to a point of interest you haven't discovered on that island → it stays on your map for good

    DiscoveredT-StonesAlso
    Warp Hole · Courier Warp Hole400Survival EXP by island level
    Crater · ground rift400Survival EXP by island level
    Rift (Warp Accelerator)400Survival EXP by island level
    Harbor · faction spot—Survival EXP by island level
    Every point on the island4,000 (claim once per island)—
    • Only the first discovery of each point counts
    • The map's Landmarks Found counter now counts craters too, whether you walked to them or revealed them with a map voucher — islands that were stuck one short (e.g. 4/5) show the full count the next time you open the map, and you can claim the full-exploration reward
    • Claiming the full reward before finding everything = «ยังสำรวจจุดสำคัญไม่ครบ» (not all points found yet)
    • An Unstable Island that expires and respawns counts as a new island — discovered markers, fog and the full-exploration reward start over, so you can discover and claim again (Island lifespan)
    • Tamed Islands (all 5 layouts): you know all 5 warp holes the moment you arrive and can warp at once, and there is no T-Stone reward for discovering or for finding everything (Warps & Rifts)

    #Return to Camp button ✓ Live

    Open the map and press Return to Camp to warp back to the camp of the island you are on — free, a 2-second warp wait like warping to the harbor, no cooldown.

    • The button shows on the Safe House and on unstable islands that have a camp · islands without a camp have no such button
    • You land next to the camp's warp hole (on the side facing the Communication Center) · a camp with no warp hole lands you next to its campfire
    • It cannot be used while dead, in combat mode, or while sailing out of the island
    • If you die on an unstable island that has a camp and choose "Revive at Camp", you respawn at that same spot — Death & Revival

    #Map vouchers ✓ Live

    Use a map voucher to reveal the whole island map without walking it · vouchers are kept separately in your wallet (Market and Shop) · on Unstable Islands an opened map lasts until the island expires — the new island needs a new one

    #Encyclopedia ✓ Live

    • Farming encyclopedia — each seed has its own level (max 20), +20 EXP per harvest · pick a specialization every 5 levels — details on Farming
    • Discovering a new animal species for the first time gives Survival EXP (Skills and Careers)

    #Memos ⏳ Not ready

    • Each successful natural-item gather has a 35% chance to give you one memo from a set you have not finished on that island (the story reads in order) — a corner notice appears and you can read it in the Encyclopedia · only gathering natural items counts (not butchering, farm harvests, or water spots/traps) · Tamed Islands do not drop memos
    • Faction memos unlock one by one when your friendship points with the faction reach that memo's requirement — you start with 16 already unlocked (Organizations)
    • In total: 505 gathering memos + 342 faction memos

    💡 Tip

    Every island except Tamed Islands pays 400 per point + 4,000 when complete — fully exploring your first islands is a great T-Stone source for new players
