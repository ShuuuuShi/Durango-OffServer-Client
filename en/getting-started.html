    Getting Started ✓ Live

    Create a character, finish the tutorial on Ancora, build a raft to the Safe House, do the 6 Company missions and take your first tasks from K

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Every new character starts on Ancora — the tutorial island that teaches the first survival steps. Once your Raft is finished you sail to the Safe House, your first base. From there you head out to your Tamed Island and the other islands.

    #What you can do

    • Create up to 3 characters per account
    • Watch the prologue or press Skip
    • Pick your character's former job at creation — it gives starting skills and items
    • Learn the basics on Ancora: scanning, gathering, resting, eating
    • Build a raft and depart for the Safe House
    • Do the 6 Company missions at the Safe House's Communication Center, then take tasks from K and the beginner pioneer tasks that pay T Stone
    • From the Safe House on, press the To Camp button on the map to warp back to camp
    • Tap other players to view their profile, message them, or invite them to a party/clan

    #How to start

    #1. Create a character ✓ Live

    1. Launch the game → Select Server (the server list is shown in game · see Picking a server) → Create Character
    2. Set your appearance, then pick your character's former job in the prologue — it hands out starting skills and items on your first login (see Skills & jobs)
    3. You can Skip the opening scene
    • One account holds 3 characters — characters waiting to be deleted still count
    • Deleting a character: it is removed for real after a 24-hour countdown, which you can cancel · a character that is online cannot be deleted
    • For logging in and moving to another PC see Account & login

    #2. The tutorial on Ancora ✓ Live

    New characters spawn on Ancora. The guide pops up step by step, and the tasks (the "Ancora" group) run in order:

    StepTaskGoalReward
    1Try the scan buttonPress the magnifier button in the screen corner—
    2Logs for the raftHave 4 Logs—
    3Stalks to bind the raftHave 6 Stalks—
    4Leave AncoraPut the materials into the raft on the beach and depart to the Safe House500 T Stone

    The tasks on Ancora give no EXP — your levels come from doing things: on this island each action gives a fixed amount of EXP

    Action on AncoraEXP
    Gathering2
    Gathering that yields a log (chopping wood)5
    Crafting10
    Killing an animal4
    Discovering · taming0

    Make a Raft: touch the raft on the beach → put in 4 Logs + 6 Stalks → when complete press Depart → you land at the Safe House

    Starting condition: a new character gets up after K's first aid injured — Life 200/300 and Energy 50/100 · K gives you 1 Survival Food (if you have nothing edible in your bag): eating it refills Energy to 100 and shows the "Full" state for about 5 seconds · on Ancora every level-up refills your Life (elsewhere it recovers as usual) · characters created before this change are not affected

    • The raft is the only way off Ancora — sailing any other way before the raft is done is refused
    • The date palms for the food task grow new fruit in 5 seconds, so nobody gets stuck waiting
    • If you log out mid-tutorial you come back to the same spot on Ancora, with your Life/Energy as you left them

    What's at each tutorial spot — Ancora's resources were rebuilt to match the original game. Everything is on ground you can walk to; nothing sits on cliffs or unreachable plateaus.

    SpotWhat you'll find
    Old railroad carsDate palms · 2 small Compsognathus (scenery)
    The streamReeds
    The lakeshoreStones · reeds (the Brachiosaurus stay in the lake — none walk on land)
    The bonfireBranches · stones
    The small lake by the raftTrees for logs · reeds
    • Resting at the bonfire on Ancora empties your Fatigue in 5 seconds (only bonfires on this island · Life/Health recover at the normal rate)
    • Wood/stone/plants on this island are as plentiful and come back as fast as on other islands (no special multiplier anymore)
    • Scenery from the 2019 original: by the bonfire there is an old road clearing, a pile of tires and Charlie standing around · the beach has a survivors' scene (a small campfire, survivors, scaffolding, an animal carcass) · the other survivors moved next to the raft · there is a second date palm, more nest ferns and reeds along the stream, and clams on the beach
    • At the bonfire Charlie asks you to use the "Shh" emoticon (not the smile) — it sits on the last page of the emoticon bar and the arrow takes you to that page · needs game 3.1.5 or newer

    #Your first axe ✓ Live

    The tutorial's axe step uses the Improvised One-Handed Axe recipe, which is unlocked from the moment you create your character — no skill level and no bone/skull needed. Put in a Stone Blade (made from stones) + a Reed Stalk + a branch to get an Improvised Stone Axe, then chop trees for the raft logs.

    • Characters created before this change get the recipe automatically on their next login

    💡 Tip

    No axe yet? Look for fallen logs on the ground, and reeds along the stream or the lakes.

    #3. The Safe House — your first base ✓ Live

    The raft always lands you at the Safe House's entry point (the Company's rest island). It is a safe island: no fatigue from climate or island level, and the weather is mostly sunny (see Weather).

    • If you log in stuck in deep water and can't move (on any island), the server moves you to the island's entry point · a return point left in deep water is cleared, so dying takes you to the entry point instead · players on land, beaches or shallow water stay where they are

    What you can do at the Safe House:

    • Do the 6 Company missions at the Communication Center (next heading)
    • Talk to K to get the "Tasks from K" chain (after all 6 Company missions)
    • Take the balloon to pick your own Tamed Island — choose 1 of 5 maps, once (see Choosing your Tamed Island layout)
    • Sail to other islands from the port (see Travel)
    • Press the To Camp button on the map to warp back to camp
    • It is the default return point when the island you were on is not stable (see Death)

    #Company missions at the Communication Center ✓ Live

    The in-game guide sends you to the camp's Communication Center, which offers the 6 Company missions one at a time, in order (the next one is offered once you finish the current one) — the arrow in front of your character points to the target area, and items you must hand in go into the camp storage.

    StepMissionGoalReward
    1Hunt the CompsognathusGo to the meadow west of the camp · kill 2 Compsognathus · collect 1 Meat60 T Stone · 46 EXP
    2Hunt the Timid RaptorsGo to the woods east of the camp · kill 2 Timid Raptors · collect 1 Bone and hand it in at the camp storage60 T Stone · 46 EXP · Branch ×1 · Meat ×2
    3Skewers over the campfireCook 2 skewers at the camp bonfire and hand in 160 T Stone · 46 EXP · Stone Pickaxe ×1
    4Stones for the CompanyCollect 3 stones and hand them in at the camp storage60 T Stone · 46 EXP
    5Leaf OutfitMake 2 Leaf Outfits and hand in 1 (wear the other)60 T Stone · 46 EXP
    6Fuel for the balloonCollect 5 flammable items and hand them in at the camp storage60 T Stone · 46 EXP

    (Mission names appear in Thai in game.)

    • On missions 1 and 2, if the hunt step makes no progress for about 4 minutes an Instant Completion button appears so you can skip the hunt step
    • Tutorial rewards don't count toward the daily mission reward cap and don't use up the first-daily-mission bonus
    • Handing in the mission 6 fuel at the camp storage makes the "A small step" quest (take the balloon to your Tamed Island) appear on screen at once (Tasks & story)
    • There are 2 small packs of Timid Raptors east of the camp (they run away and don't enter the camp) · the area around K's motorbike and balloon is K's land, signed "K's Domain" — you can't declare land over it or build there, but the balloon and motorbike work as normal
    • Characters that already passed the Safe House don't have to do these missions

    #The "To Camp" button on the map ✓ Live

    Open the map and press To Camp → wait 2 seconds for the warp (same as warping to the port) and you land next to the camp's warphole (next to the camp bonfire if the camp has no warphole) — free, no cooldown.

    • The button shows on the Safe House and on unstable islands that have a camp · islands without a camp have no button
    • It can't be used in combat, while dead, or while sailing away from the island
    • If you die on an unstable island with a camp, the revive button becomes Revive at Camp and you revive at the camp (you can still pick a warphole on the map instead)

    #5. Welcome mail ✓ Live

    A new character receives a welcome mail — the first character of an account gets 300 T Stone attached (characters 2–3 get the text only). Update-news mails with attachments are also paid once per account. See Mail.

    #Tapping other players ✓ Live

    Tapping another player's character opens a menu:

    ButtonWhat it does
    View ProfileTheir name, level and clan
    MessageOpens a private two-person chat · players who block each other can't message
    Invite to PartyGreyed out if you are a member (not the leader), the party is full (8), or they are already in another party
    Invite to ClanShown only when your clan rank can invite and they have no clan
    ReportTell the team — type /report reason in chat after tapping that player (within 10 minutes), or /report <character name> <reason> · you can report the same player once a day · the server's reply is in Thai
    • Tapping a downed character still gives CPR as before (Death)

    #Name tags: country flag + phone/PC ✓ Live

    Player names show a country flag and whether they play on mobile or PC — on the name above characters, in chat and whispers, in friend/clan member lists and in rankings.

    Game 3.1.5 or newer (the minimum version that can enter the server) shows flag images + phone/PC icons.

    • The country is detected from your connection every time you log in · the server keeps only the 2-letter country code, never your IP address
    • Set it yourself in chat: /flag us (any 2-letter country code) · /flag auto detect (default) · /flag off hide · /flag show current — people around you see it right away; your own head tag updates after you log in again
    • Real character names don't change — search, add friends, whisper and report with the real name (pasting a name with tags also works)
    • Country data: IP Geolocation by DB-IP (db-ip.com, CC-BY 4.0) · flag/icon images in 3.1.5: Twemoji (CC-BY 4.0)

    #Party, chat and favorite islands ⏳ Not ready

    • Party chat channel: what you type in the Party channel reaches the leader and every member who has accepted the invite, even on other islands (before, only you saw it) · players who were invited but haven't accepted, who left the party, or outsiders don't see it · when you log back in you still see the party's latest messages (50 · a party that stays silent for more than 6 hours has its history cleared) · typing too fast hits the same quota as other channels
    • Headcount on chat channels: a number appears after the channel name (island, clan, party) showing how many people are in that channel on all islands, updated every 2 seconds — the island channel counts the people on the same island
    • Group whisper rooms: inviting someone or someone leaving no longer pops the chat window again, and your local history is kept
    • Favorite islands: keep up to 20 Tamed Island owners you like (not yourself) — the server remembers them after you log out
