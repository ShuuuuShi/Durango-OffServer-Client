    Mail ✓ Live

    Your mailbox receives gifts, update news, unsold Market items and items from other players — collect everything at once with Receive All.

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Mail is where items and news from the server arrive — welcome gifts, update news with attachments, unsold Market items, and items other players send you. Attached items, T-Stones and tickets are not added to your bag until you receive them.

    #What you can do

    • Open and read mail
    • Receive attachments one mail at a time, or Receive All
    • Delete mail you have already collected, or that has no attachments

    #Mailbox ✓ Live

    Press the mail icon on the main screen — a notification dot appears when new mail arrives, and the server sends your mailbox as soon as you log in. The mailbox has 2 sides: System (mail from the server) and Player (items other players sent you).

    1. Select a mail → read it
    2. Press Receive — items go to your bag, T-Stones/tickets to your wallet
    3. Press Delete — only mail with nothing left to collect can be deleted (otherwise you're told to receive it first)
    • Attached items are the actual items — you get their durability, color and tags as they were

    #Receive All ✓ Live

    The Receive All button collects every automatic mail at once (welcome, update news, gifts). Mail sent by the staff by hand is highlighted — open it first, then Receive All will include it.

    ℹ️ Note

    Older update news published before your server opened may not be sent to you — each server only sends the news that applies to it.

    #Expiry and mailbox size ✓ Live

    ItemValue
    Normal mail lifetime7 days
    Update news lifetimeusually 14 days
    Mailbox holds100 mails
    • Expired mail that is already collected or has no attachments is cleared out
    • When the mailbox is full and new mail arrives, the server removes old collected/read mail first — mail with items still waiting is never thrown away

    💡 Tip

    • Collect gifts soon; don't wait until they expire
    • If your mailbox is very full, the game may only show the newest mail — collect and delete old mail to see the rest

    #Mail from players ✓ Live

    The Player side receives items other players send you, e.g. a thank-you gift after you revive someone — if you are not online at that moment, it comes by mail instead, so nothing is lost.
