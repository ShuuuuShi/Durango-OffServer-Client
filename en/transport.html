    Transport and Vehicles ✓ Live

    Ship items home through Courier Warp Holes, board a catapult, fly a balloon and ride animals — for travelling between islands see the Islands page.

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    This page covers moving goods and vehicles on this server.

    • Send items home from any island → Courier Warp Holes
    • Vehicles available now → the Catapult (boarding only) · balloons at camps · riding pets
    • Travelling to other islands → see Islands and Warp

    #Shipping items home (Courier Warp Holes) ✓ Live

    No need to carry loot home yourself — send it from a Warp Hole to an exit you built.

    1. Learn a Courier Warp Hole (Exit) blueprint through skills (see Skills) and build it near your home
    2. Touch the finished exit → activate it — it becomes your personal destination (1 at a time · it must be one you built)
    3. Go to any Warp Hole — the neutral warp holes on islands, camp warp holes, or warp holes built by players — stand close and choose to send items
    4. Pick items from your bag (equipped items can't be sent) → pay the fee in T-Stones → the items leave your bag
    5. Wait for the arrival time, then take them out of the exit's box · to get them now, press receive immediately (extra cost)
    ExitFootprintCapacity (total item size)
    Makeshift Courier Warp Hole (Exit)2×220
    Basic Courier Warp Hole (Exit)3×345
    Journeyman Courier Warp Hole (Exit)3×345
    Expert Courier Warp Hole (Exit)4×480

    ℹ️ Note

    The in-game description says 80/120/160/200 slots, but this server counts capacity as in the table above (items on the way + items already in the box must fit together).

    ItemValue
    Shipping fee30 T-Stones × total size of the shipment
    Arrival time60 seconds × total size of the shipment
    Receive immediately(minutes left, rounded down, + 1) × 1 T-Stone

    💡 Tip

    • Big items have a large size, so fee and time go up — small items ship cheap and fast
    • If the exit's box is full, arrived items wait until there is room — empty it often
    • If you dismantle your exit, build a new one and activate it again

    #Catapult ✓ Live

    The only vehicle you can sit in on this server is the Catapult — touch it and press Board to get on, Dismount to get off. Travelling to another island, dying or disconnecting dismounts you automatically. There is no Catapult blueprint to learn yet (only ones placed by admins/events), and it cannot fire yet.

    #Balloons ✓ Live

    Some camps have a balloon station — touch it to board for free and fly freely over water and mountains · you can only land at spots where landing is allowed.

    ItemValue
    Longest flight15 minutes (then you land automatically)
    Costfree

    ℹ️ Note

    If you disconnect in mid-air, you come back at the balloon station when you log in again.
