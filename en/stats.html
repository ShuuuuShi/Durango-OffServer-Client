    Character stats ✓ Live

    The 8 basic abilities, max Health/Energy, combat stats, crafting/gathering stats, resistances and power ratings — where they come from, how they're calculated, which ones really matter

    The character info screen shows many values — the numbers it shows are now the same values the server really uses, counting every source (skills · titles · gear and tags · food · house ambiance). This page explains where each one comes from, how it's calculated and what it does on this server.

    ValueComes fromAffects
    8 basic abilitiesCharacter level + skill category levels + title + food/gearEndurance → max Health · Will → max Energy
    Max Health / Energy · regen per secondBase + level + basic abilities + bonuses from every sourceThe on-screen gauges (Survival)
    Combat statsThe weapon and gear you wear + character bonuses · skill effects on movesDamage dealt and taken · auto-dodge/auto-block (Combat)
    Crafting / gathering statsCharacter level + learned skills + bonuses from every sourceMastery ladder · craft/gather success chance (Crafting · Gathering)
    Climate resistanceTime spent on islands of each climate + gearEntry gates for high-Index islands · less weather fatigue (Weather)
    3 power ratingsWeighted from the values aboveDisplay only

    #The 8 basic abilities ✓ Live

    Ability = 10 + 0.5 × (character level − 1) + 0.35 × sum of (feeding category level − 1) + title bonus + food/gear/tag bonus · minimum 1, maximum 100 · the character screen rounds to whole numbers

    AbilityFed by skill categories
    StrengthMelee · Construction
    EnduranceDefense · Survival
    AgilityMelee · Ranged
    DexterityWeapon/Tool · Tailoring · Processing
    PerceptionGathering · Ranged
    IntelligenceCooking · Processing
    WillSurvival · Butchering
    CharismaCooking · Tailoring

    Example: a Lv30 character with Survival Lv20 and Defense Lv10 → Endurance = 10 + 14.5 + 0.35 × (19 + 9) = 34.3

    ℹ️ Note

    The basic abilities that actually do something are Endurance (max Health) and Will (max Energy) — Endurance/Will from food, tags and gear raise the gauges too · the other 6 are shown on the character screen but aren't used in any damage or chance calculation yet

    #Max Health and Energy ✓ Live

    ValueFormula
    Max Health (ceiling of Life)300 + 4 × (level − 1) + 2 × (Endurance − 10) + max Health bonus from every source
    Max Energy (ceiling of Stamina)100 + 1.5 × (level − 1) + 0.8 × (Will − 10) + max Energy bonus from every source

    "Every source" = skills · titles · tags and built-in bonuses of the gear you wear · food · house ambiance — the on-screen gauge always equals the number on the character screen

    • Max Health goes up → your current Life rises with it right away (like a level-up)
    • Max Energy bonus from skills/gear/house ambiance → only widens the gauge, it doesn't refill Energy for free (eat or wait for it to regenerate)
    • Take gear off or a buff ends and the maximum drops → the current value is cut down to the new maximum
    • The Fatigue maximum can also be raised by food, gear, titles and house ambiance

    Example: a Lv30 character with 472 max Health learns all 6 max-Health skill levels (+25/+25/+50/+50/+50/+50 = +250) → max Health 722 · the max-Energy skill has 10 levels (+10 each) = +100

    Level (no skill categories or bonuses yet)Basic abilityMax HealthMax Energy
    110300100
    1014.5345117
    3024.5445155
    6039.5595212

    Skill categories that feed Endurance/Will push these higher — e.g. Lv60 with Survival and Defense both at Lv60 → Endurance = 39.5 + 0.35 × 118 = 80.8 → Max Health ≈ 678

    • Hypothermia/overheating lowers Health temporarily (Survival)
    • The Fatigue gauge tops out at 100 · warning at 60, danger at 90

    #Regeneration per second ✓ Live

    Regeneration bonuses from every source (skills · titles · gear · house ambiance) add to how fast each gauge refills by itself

    GaugeBase regenerationExample bonus
    Life1.0/secondAll 6 Life-regen skill levels (+0.1/+0.1/+0.1/+0.2/+0.2/+0.3) → 2.0/second
    Stamina5.0/secondAll 6 Stamina-regen skill levels (+0.25/+0.25/+0.5/+0.5/+0.5/+0.5) → 7.5/second
    Energy+16.7/hourCute Ambiance in your house +0.15/second (= +9/min)

    While dead, gauges don't regenerate

    #Combat stats ✓ Live

    The combat stats on the character screen are the values used when you hit and get hit = what you hold/wear + your character bonuses (skills · titles · tags and built-in gear bonuses · food · house ambiance) — the full formula is on Combat

    StatIn real combat
    AttackThe attack of the weapon in your hand (bare hands = 40) + attack bonuses · a weapon with 0 Durability gives nothing
    Armor penetrationThe weapon's (bare hands = 40) + penetration bonuses — the further above the animal's defense, the harder you hit
    DefenseDefense of every clothing/accessory piece you wear (pieces at 0 Durability don't count) + defense bonuses (e.g. the 6 Defense skill levels +10/+10/+10/+20/+20/+30 = +100)
    Critical HitThe move's critical chance 10% (heavy moves 18%) + 1% per Critical point · damage ×1.9
    AccuracyThe move's hit chance (normal moves 95%) + 0.1% per Accuracy point above 100 (below 100 lowers it)
    EvasionAuto-dodge chance = Evasion × 0.1%, max 15% — the animal's hit shows «Dodged» and you lose no health (Combat)
    Auto-blockHolding a melee weapon → 5% + 0.25% per Defense category level (max 20%) to reduce the damage you take (Combat)
    Skill effects on movesLearned skills unlock new moves and boost existing ones · armor-penetration skills give moves a chance for that hit to ignore the animal's defense
    • Material tags and built-in gear bonuses (e.g. «attack up», «defense up», «evasion») are already counted in the values above (Item attributes)
    • The accuracy value on weapons themselves is not used (only your character's Accuracy)
    • Weapons wear with the damage they deal · gear wears with the damage you take — repair on Equipment and repair

    #Crafting and gathering stats ✓ Live

    Each branch's mastery value (character screen) = character level + 4 × skill nodes learned in the feeding categories + bonuses from every source (food · gear and tags · titles · house ambiance · mastery ladder) — the value on screen is the value the server really uses

    BranchFed by skill categories
    Weapon CraftingWeapon/Tool
    Armor craftingTailoring
    SewingTailoring · Processing
    SmithingProcessing · Weapon/Tool
    CookingCooking
    Furniture CraftingConstruction · Processing
    ConstructionConstruction
    FarmingFarming
    HandicraftProcessing
    Gathering · MiningGathering
    ButcheringButchering
    • These values are compared against the mastery ladder — reach a threshold and you get extra recipes/blueprints automatically · steps you already reached stay when you take gear off (Crafting)
    • Recipe screens show the required value as difficulty · this server does not block crafting if you're below it, but the further below the difficulty, the lower your success chance — the estimate screen shows the real chance (Crafting)
    • Gathering values from non-skill sources (titles, tags, gear, food) add 0.5% gathering success chance per point (Gathering)

    #Climate resistance ✓ Live

    Resistance to each climate (swamp, desert, tropical forest, volcano, snowfield, tundra) grows by itself while you stay on islands of that climate and gates high-Index islands — details on Weather · temperate forest/grassland islands use the highest resistance level you have

    #Resistance from gear ✓ Live

    Resistance values (heat · cold · humidity · wind etc.) from tags, gear, status effects, titles and food are shown on the survival panel and reduce fatigue from that kind of weather/climate: fatigue × (1 − resistance ÷ 100) — resistance 100 = no fatigue from that kind at all · resistance of the opposite kind (e.g. cold resistance while somewhere hot) makes you tire faster × (1 + value ÷ 100), up to ×2 · this does not affect island entry gates (Weather)

    #Power ratings ✓ Live

    The character screen shows Combat Power · Crafting Power · Gathering Power as summary numbers — for comparison only, they don't affect the game

    PowerWeighted from
    Combat PowerEvasion · Defense · Attack · Accuracy · Critical Hit · Armor penetration
    Crafting PowerAll 9 crafting branches (equal weight)
    Gathering PowerGathering · Mining · Butchering

    #Ways to raise your stats

    • Character level — every level raises basic abilities, Health, Energy and crafting stats (Skills and Careers)
    • Skill category levels — use that category a lot · the Defense category also raises your auto-block chance (Skills and Careers)
    • Learning skills — raises crafting stats, Defense, max Health/Energy, regeneration, how many animals you can own, and specific move effects (Skill list)
    • Titles — wear a title with a bonus (Titles)
    • Gear — weapons, clothing, their tag attributes and each piece's built-in bonuses (pieces at 0 Durability give nothing) (Equipment and repair)
    • Food and buffs — food buffs and house ambiance (Buffs and status effects)
