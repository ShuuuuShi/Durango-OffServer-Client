    Character stats ✓ Live

    The 8 basic abilities, max Health/Energy, combat stats, crafting/gathering stats, resistances and power ratings — where they come from, how they're calculated, which ones really matter

    The character info screen shows many values — some directly affect play, some are display only. This page explains where each one comes from, how it's calculated and what it does on this server.

    ValueComes fromAffects
    8 basic abilitiesCharacter level + skill category levels + titleEndurance → max Health · Will → max Energy
    Max Health / EnergyBase + level + basic abilities + titleThe on-screen gauges (Survival)
    Combat statsThe weapon and gear you wear · skill effects on movesDamage dealt and taken (Combat)
    Crafting / gathering statsCharacter level + learned skills + bonusesUnlocking recipes on the mastery ladder (Crafting)
    Climate resistanceTime spent on islands of each climateEntry gates for high-Index islands (Weather)
    3 power ratingsWeighted from the values aboveDisplay only

    #The 8 basic abilities ✓ Live

    Ability = 10 + 0.5 × (character level − 1) + 0.35 × sum of (feeding category level − 1) + title bonus · minimum 1, maximum 100 · the character screen rounds to whole numbers

    AbilityFed by skill categories
    StrengthMelee · Construction
    EnduranceDefense · Survival
    AgilityMelee · Ranged
    DexterityWeapon/Tool · Tailoring · Processing
    PerceptionGathering · Ranged
    IntelligenceCooking · Processing
    WillSurvival · Butchering
    CharismaCooking · Tailoring

    Example: a Lv30 character with Survival Lv20 and Defense Lv10 → Endurance = 10 + 14.5 + 0.35 × (19 + 9) = 34.3

    ℹ️ Note

    On this server the basic abilities that actually do something are Endurance (max Health) and Will (max Energy) — the other 6 are shown on the character screen but aren't used in any damage or chance calculation yet

    #Max Health and Energy ✓ Live

    ValueFormula
    Max Health (ceiling of Life)300 + 4 × (level − 1) + 2 × (Endurance − 10) + title bonus
    Max Energy (ceiling of Stamina)100 + 1.5 × (level − 1) + 0.8 × (Will − 10) + title bonus
    Level (no skill categories yet)Basic abilityMax HealthMax Energy
    110300100
    1014.5345117
    3024.5445155
    6039.5595212

    Skill categories that feed Endurance/Will push these higher — e.g. Lv60 with Survival and Defense both at Lv60 → Endurance = 39.5 + 0.35 × 118 = 80.8 → Max Health ≈ 678

    • Hypothermia/overheating lowers Health temporarily (Survival)
    • The Fatigue gauge tops out at 100 · warning at 60, danger at 90

    #Combat stats ✓ Live

    Real damage comes from what you hold and wear, not from basic abilities — the full formula is on Combat

    StatIn real combat
    AttackThe attack of the weapon in your hand (bare hands = 40) · a weapon with 0 Durability gives nothing
    Armor penetrationThe weapon's (bare hands = 40) — the further above the animal's defense, the harder you hit
    DefenseSum of the defense of every clothing/accessory piece you wear (pieces at 0 Durability don't count)
    Critical HitFixed 10% chance (heavy moves 18%), damage ×1.9
    AccuracyHit chance comes from the move's accuracy (normal moves 95%)
    Skill effects on movesLearned skills unlock new moves and boost existing ones
    • Material tags (e.g. «attack up», «defense up») add to the weapon/gear by level (Item attributes)
    • Weapons wear with the damage they deal · gear wears with the damage you take — repair on Equipment and repair

    #Crafting and gathering stats ✓ Live

    Each branch's mastery value (character screen) = character level + 4 × skill nodes learned in the feeding categories + bonuses (food, gear, titles, mastery ladder)

    BranchFed by skill categories
    Weapon CraftingWeapon/Tool
    Armor craftingTailoring
    SewingTailoring · Processing
    SmithingProcessing · Weapon/Tool
    CookingCooking
    Furniture CraftingConstruction · Processing
    ConstructionConstruction
    FarmingFarming
    HandicraftProcessing
    Gathering · MiningGathering
    ButcheringButchering
    • These values are compared against the mastery ladder — reach a threshold and you get extra recipes/blueprints automatically (Crafting)
    • Recipe screens show the required value as difficulty, but this server does not block crafting if you're below it

    #Climate resistance ✓ Live

    Resistance to each climate (swamp, desert, tropical forest, volcano, snowfield, tundra) grows by itself while you stay on islands of that climate and gates high-Index islands — details on Weather · temperate forest/grassland islands use the highest resistance level you have

    #Power ratings ✓ Live

    The character screen shows Combat Power · Crafting Power · Gathering Power as summary numbers — for comparison only, they don't affect the game

    PowerWeighted from
    Combat PowerEvasion · Defense · Attack · Accuracy · Critical Hit · Armor penetration
    Crafting PowerAll 9 crafting branches (equal weight)
    Gathering PowerGathering · Mining · Butchering

    #Ways to raise your stats

    • Character level — every level raises basic abilities, Health, Energy and crafting stats (Skills and Careers)
    • Skill category levels — use that category a lot (Skills and Careers)
    • Learning skills — raises crafting stats and gives specific effects (Skill list)
    • Titles — wear a title with a bonus (Titles)
    • Gear — weapons, clothing and their tag attributes (Equipment and repair)
    • Food and buffs — food buffs and house moods (Buffs and status effects)
