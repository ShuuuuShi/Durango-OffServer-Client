    Taming ✓ Live

    Beat an animal below 30% health or stun it, then capture it with a Taming Herb — tame it in a pen before forming a bond

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Almost every wild animal can become your companion. The steps are weaken it → capture it with a Taming Herb → get its Rein → tame it in a pen → form a bond, and you get a pet that travels with you, can be ridden, helps in fights and produces goods.

    #What you can do

    • Capture a wild animal at ≤ 30% health or while it is stunned/collapsed (any health)
    • Use blunt weapons or heavy moves to stun animals faster
    • Tame captured animals in a taming pen, then bond with them as pets

    #What you need ✓ Live

    ItemLevelUsed for
    Novice Taming Herb1–19Capture tool — must be in your bag
    Standard Taming Herb20–39〃
    Advanced Taming Herb40–60〃
    Makeshift Taming Pen / Small Taming Pen—Taming the animals you capture
    • The capture tool loses 3 durability per capture attempt (success or not) · pressing capture before the animal qualifies does not wear the tool

    #How to capture ✓ Live

    1. Hit the animal until it has ≤ 30% health or until it is stunned/collapsed (see the stun gauge below)
    2. Target it and press Capture — the button is always pressable when you have a tool; the server checks the conditions
    3. Stand still for the 3-second bar (moving cancels)
    4. Success → the animal leaves the world and its Rein goes into your bag · failure → a message shows the chance that was rolled; try again after the 5-second cooldown
    • If it doesn't qualify yet, a server message (in Thai) tells you to get its health below 30% or stun it first
    • Carcasses and rift guardians can't be captured · you must stand close · some species can't be captured at all (button locked)

    💡 Tip

    If you walk away from a low-health animal it can heal back above 30% (it regenerates 20 seconds after combat) — capture quickly

    #Capture chance ✓ Live

    Chance = level factor × (1 − 0.8 × (remaining health ÷ max health)²) · always between 2% and 98%

    • The level factor comes from your character level − the animal's level; the higher you are, the easier · 6 or more levels below the animal = 2%
    • Being stunned/collapsed only allows capture at high health — the chance still uses the real health, with no extra bonus

    #Stun gauge ✓ Live

    Every animal has a stun gauge (for small animals it is roughly 2× their health · multiplied by the island's Unstable Index). Each hit drains it, and the animal moves through these stages

    StageGauge leftEffectIn-game message
    Weakened≤ ⅓Warning message only"… is losing strength."
    Stunned≤ ⅑Frozen: no attacks, no walking, no fleeing · the attack it was winding up is cancelled"… is stunned."
    Collapsed0Falls down with stars over its head"… collapsed!"
    Recoveredtime runs outGauge full again"… steadied itself."
    • A heavy hit can skip from weakened straight to collapsed · you can capture at any moment while it's stunned/collapsed
    • When the fight ends (the animal gives up or returns home) the gauge silently refills · the gauge does not recover by itself mid-fight

    #Stunning faster ✓ Live

    Gauge drained per hit = damage dealt × the move's stun value × direction (front/back ×1.25 · sides ×1) × (1 + skill bonus) × 4

    MoveStun value
    Bow/crossbow normal shot · quick/aimed shot0.3 · 0.5
    Bare hand · one-handed · two-handed (normal hit)0.4 · 0.7 · 0.8
    One-handed heavy hit · two-handed heavy hit0.8 · 1.0
    One-handed stab · two-handed strike1.5 · 1.6
    • Blunt Weapon Specialization I–III +6% per level (blunt weapon moves) · Crossbow Specialization I–III +10% per level
    • No drain from: your pet's attacks · bleeding · animals fighting each other · traps

    Hits needed to stun / collapse / kill (hitting from the front, weapon level = animal level · "dies first" = health runs out before that stage)

    Bare HandedStunned 13 · dies 14 (dies before collapsing)83 · 93 · 172
    Stone Hammer3 · 4 · 610 · 11 · 34
    Two-Handed Stone Hammer strike + Blunt Weapon Specialization III1 · 1 · 21 · 2 · 10
    Mahogany Crossbow + Crossbow Specialization IIIDies before stun (dies at 6)16 · 18 · 33

    💡 Tip

    For small animals use a hammer or other blunt weapon and hit from the front or back — they usually get stunned while still at 30–45% health, so you can capture before accidentally killing them

    #After the capture: taming in a pen ✓ Live

    1. Build/place a taming pen and put the Rein into it
    2. Start taming — it takes 60 seconds · base success chance 60% (maximum 100%)
    3. Feed it during taming to help:
    FoodEffect
    All-Purpose Taming FeedChance +10% · time −30%
    Fast Taming FeedTime −50%
    Meat / MinnowTime −10 seconds per piece
    1. When time is up, collect the result: success → the animal is tamed and gets a random rank (S · A · B · C · D, whichever that species can have) · failure → the animal escapes to the wild and the Rein is lost
    2. Use the tamed animal's Rein to form a bond → it becomes your pet (the animal's level must not exceed your Survival category level) — continue at Pets
    • Taming time can't be cut below half

    ℹ️ Note

    Animal traps give carcass items (meat, leather, bones), not live animals to raise
