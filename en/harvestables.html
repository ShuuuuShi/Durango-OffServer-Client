    Traps and wells ✓ Live

    Structures that produce items by themselves — wells, fish traps, lizard bait, planted trees/bushes — what you get, how many per round, how long until it refills, where the blueprint comes from

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Some structures refill with items in rounds — build one on your Domain, then come back and touch it to collect, just like gathering in the wild (Gathering) · each item has a per-round quota; once you take it all, wait for it to come back · build them like any other structure (Building)

    • Items you get are the level of the island the structure stands on · collecting restarts the refill timer of that item
    • The structure must be finished and not broken (Decay and repair)

    #Not obtainable yet: animal traps ⏳ Not ready

    The animal trap system (Basket Trap · Avalanche Trap · Spike Pit) already exists on the server — place one and it catches wild animals that walk past (level up to the trap's level +10 to +20 depending on the type), or produces lure catches when no animal passes for a while (meat, hide, bone, feathers, eggs, tendon) · you get carcass items, not live animals · but no skill or reward unlocks the animal trap blueprints yet, so they can't be built
