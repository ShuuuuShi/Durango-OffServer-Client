    Eating and drinking ✓ Live

    Eat to refill energy and get buffs and taste bonuses — a hidden satiety meter stops you from binge-eating

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Food is your character's main source of Energy. Besides energy, some foods restore health, give stat buffs, help against heat or cold, or boost the exp you earn when they taste good — but you can't binge-eat, because a hidden "satiety" meter holds you back.

    #What you can do

    • Eat food from your bag → get its full energy right away, plus the food's other effects
    • Drink Water at a river, lake or sea shore without needing a bottle or item
    • Eat tasty food to get the Delicious status (more exp) and foods that give the Enhanced Ability buff
    • Cook before eating to avoid Stomach Pain from raw food (see Cooking)

    #How to eat and drink

    1. Open your bag, pick a food and choose Eat (drinks: Drink) — your character plays an eating animation, then the effect applies
    2. Stand next to water and choose Drink Water — takes 5 seconds and gives +5 energy and the Water status, same as drinking a bottle of water, without using an item
    3. If you eat a different buff food while a food buff is still active, the game asks "Previous food's effect will disappear. Continue?" — confirm and the new buff replaces the old one

    #Satiety

    There is no satiety bar on screen, but the server tracks it in the background:

    • Every food has its own satiety value, added when you eat it
    • Satiety caps at 100 and digests steadily, from 100 down to empty in 30 minutes
    • While you are below 100 you can always eat, even if that bite takes you over 100
    • Once you reach 100 you get the Full status and can't eat; the game tells you roughly how many seconds/minutes to wait
    • Dying or levelling up removes Full immediately
    • Drink Water from a water source does not count toward satiety

    #Taste

    All of a food's taste values (sweet, salty, savory, oily, spicy, bitter, sour) add up to one score:

    Taste scoreStatusEffect (5 minutes)
    3 or moreDeliciousCharacter exp from actions ×1.03
    −3 or lessHorribleMore fatigue
    In between—Nothing

    Properties on a food item change its taste — anything with the Raw property tastes much worse (raw meat = Horrible + Stomach Pain), but cooking removes Raw, so the same meat becomes Delicious. Eating raw food also cancels Delicious.

    #Other food effects

    EffectDetails
    Enhanced AbilityStat buff (e.g. Strength, Will, Agility, Max Energy) for 5–30 minutes depending on the food · one set at a time · eating the same food again refreshes it · lost on death
    Percentage recoverySome foods restore health/energy as a share of your maximum
    Hot Food / Cold FoodFood with the Hot property helps against cold (but you feel heat more in hot places) · Cold is the reverse
    Stomach PainFrom food with the Raw property, or spoiled food
    CooldownPotent Health Recovery Medicine (and a few event items) gives Overexerted for 5 minutes, during which you can't take another of the same kind
    Level limitBooster Jelly Piece and Booster Jelly can only be eaten at levels 20–55 · they give instant exp of about 3.4% and 34% of the exp needed for your current level

    #When energy runs low

    Gathering, crafting and other work cost energy. If you have less energy than an action needs, the game warns you first and lets you choose to eat first or carry on — for the energy and fatigue gauges see Survival.

    #Tips

    💡 Tip

    Grill meat with the Skewer recipe before eating — same energy, but no Stomach Pain and you get Delicious (exp ×1.03).

    💡 Tip

    High-satiety foods (Fat, steak) fill you up fast. If you need lots of energy, pick foods with high energy but low satiety, such as Apple.

    💡 Tip

    You can only have one Enhanced Ability buff at a time — don't accidentally eat a different buff food over the one you want.
