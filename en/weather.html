    Weather & Unstable Index ✓ Live

    Weather changes every 20 minutes per island · volcanic islands get Volcanic Storms (block them with gear or a hot spring) · seasons turn every 7 days · the Unstable Index (1–10) makes islands harsher and needs a Climate Resistance Level before you can sail there

    Every island has its own weather based on its terrain (tropical forests rain often, deserts bake, snow fields snow), and the weather has real effects: you walk slower, predators see you from closer, rain waters your crops, storms put out fires. Unstable islands also carry an Unstable Index: the higher it is, the more dangerous — and the more rewarding.

    #What you can do

    • Check the current weather on the on-screen clock and plan your trips
    • Let the rain water your fields · sit out storms in a shelter
    • Spot the Precursor on a volcanic island and get ready — put on storm gear or get into a hot spring
    • Pick an island by its Index on the sailing screen
    • Build up your Climate Resistance Level by staying on islands of that climate, to unlock high-Index islands

    #Walk speed and stealth ✓ Live

    • The walk-speed multipliers in the table apply to your own walk speed: normally 500 × (1 + speed bonus from worn items), up to 1000 — e.g. the Ninja Outfit (+10%) walks at 550, or 522 in the rain · for combat speed see Character stats
    • The distance predators see you from is also divided by your Stealth (up to about 1 tile shorter) — see Wild animals

    💡 Tip

    Rain is a farmer's friend — planted fields get free water every time rain starts. See Farming.

    #Volcanic Storm ✓ Live

    All 6 volcanic islands have a storm cycle: every storm is preceded by one slot (20 minutes) of Precursor, followed by one slot (20 minutes) of Volcanic Storm · each about 10% of the time — on average once every 3 hours 20 minutes per island

    PhaseStatus you getEffect
    PrecursorPrecursorStamina −5.2/second (natural regen is only +5/second)
    Volcanic StormVolcanic StormHealth −10/second (600 per minute) · walk ×0.85 · Fatigue +1.5/min · target temperature +1.5°
    • The status goes away as soon as the weather changes, you leave the island or you die (no countdown)
    • Health is the ceiling of your Life — a few dozen seconds out in the storm and the gauge is nearly gone. With no protection, leave the island or get into a hot spring as soon as you see the Precursor

    Protection (blocks both the Precursor and the storm, including the storm's fatigue)

    • Wear the Volcanic Storm Miner Outfit (crafted at the Tailoring Workbench) or the Firefighter Outfit / Firefighter Helmet → you get Volcanic Storm Immunity while worn · an item at zero durability does not protect until repaired
    • Soak in a hot spring (below)

    #Hot springs ✓ Live

    Lakes on volcanic islands are hot springs

    • Stand or walk in one → Hot Spring Dip: protects from the Volcanic Storm · Volcanic Heat fatigue −20% · lasts another 10 seconds after you step out
    • Press Wash at the spring → you get one of these at random instead of Washed: Hot Spring Bath: Health Recovery (Health +2/second for 4 minutes) or Hot Spring Bath: Attack Buff (Attack +16 for 4 minutes) · and Hot Spring Dip instead of Wet

    #Weather resistance from gear ✓ Live

    Many outfits, hats, gloves and shoes carry a resistance attribute (the leaf outfit resists heat, the leather outfit resists cold, and so on) · the total from worn items, statuses, titles and food shows on the Survival tab of the stats window · the resistance level on an item = the item's own design level + its latent attribute (Item attributes)

    • A matching resistance: 1 point = 3% less fatigue of that kind, down to at most 90% less (30 points or more)
    • The opposite resistance adds 1% fatigue of that kind per point (up to 2×) · wearing both multiplies the two effects
    • Applies to fatigue from the island's climate and the current weather · fatigue from the Unstable Index is lowered by the resistance that matches the island's climate (no opposite kind) · fatigue from the island level is not lowered
    • The same resistance also softens how climate and weather move your body temperature (Survival)
    • This is separate from the Climate Resistance Level that unlocks high-Index islands (below) — that one still grows only by staying on the island

    #Seasons ✓ Live

    The whole world runs through 4 seasons in turn, 7 days each (a 28-day cycle), changing every Thursday at 00:00 UTC (07:00 Thai time).

    SeasonWeather more / less likelyTime for plants to come backAnimal breeding

    • One in-game day = 48 real minutes

    #Unstable Index ✓ Live

    Every unstable island of level 15 and up has an Unstable Index from 1 to 10, shown on the sailing screen.

    Island typeIndex
    Areas with 4 islands (swamp, desert, temperate forest, tropical forest, indigo tropical forest, savanna, snow field, tundra, volcanic)Fixed 1 · 4 · 7 · 10, one value per island — pick the one you want on the sailing screen
    Areas with a single island (levels 15 · 20 · 25 · 30)Goes up by 1 every time the island expires and is reborn (1 → 2 → … → 10 → 1) — it does not change during the island's life

    #Climate Resistance Level ✓ Live

    To sail to an island of Index 3 or higher you need the Climate Resistance Level for that island's climate (table above). If you are short, the sailing screen shows the requirement in red and the server refuses the departure (returning to your own land or the Safe House is not checked). · Pioneer grade no longer locks islands — every grade can pick any Index as long as you pass the resistance check (the pioneer grade still raises your land size)

    How to raise it: just be on an island of that climate (while online) and it rises by itself — faster on higher-Index islands.
