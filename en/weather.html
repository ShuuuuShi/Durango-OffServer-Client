    Weather & Unstable Index ✓ Live

    Weather changes every 20 minutes per island · seasons turn every 7 days · the Unstable Index (1–10) makes islands harsher and needs a Climate Resistance Level before you can sail there

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Every island has its own weather based on its terrain (tropical forests rain often, deserts bake, snow fields snow), and the weather has real effects: you walk slower, predators see you from closer, rain waters your crops, storms put out fires. Unstable islands also carry an Unstable Index: the higher it is, the more dangerous — and the more rewarding.

    #What you can do

    • Check the current weather on the on-screen clock and plan your trips
    • Let the rain water your fields · sit out storms in a shelter
    • Pick an island by its Index on the sailing screen
    • Build up your Climate Resistance Level by staying on islands of that climate, to unlock high-Index islands

    #Weather on each island ✓ Live

    Weather changes every 20 minutes (the same weather can roll again = it lasts longer). Everyone on the same island sees the same weather. The chances depend on the island group (before seasons are applied):

    💡 Tip

    Rain is a farmer's friend — planted fields get free water every time rain starts. See Farming.

    #Seasons ✓ Live

    The whole world runs through 4 seasons in turn, 7 days each (a 28-day cycle), changing every Thursday at 00:00 UTC (07:00 Thai time).

    SeasonWeather more / less likelyTime for plants to come backAnimal breeding
    SpringRain ×1.5 · heavy rain ×1.3 · sunny ×0.85Faster (×0.77)×1.25
    SummerSunny ×1.4 · rain ×0.75 · heavy rain ×0.7Normal×1
    AutumnCloudy ×1.35 · rain ×1.2 · sunny ×0.9Slower (×1.43)×0.75
    WinterSnow ×1.5 · heavy snow ×1.4 · cloudy ×1.1 · sunny ×0.65Slower (×1.67)×0.45
    • Seasons only reweight the odds — snow still falls only on islands that already have snow (tundra/snow field); deserts get no snow in winter
    • One in-game day = 48 real minutes

    #Unstable Index ✓ Live

    Every unstable island of level 15 and up has an Unstable Index from 1 to 10, shown on the sailing screen.

    Island typeIndex
    Areas with 4 islands (swamp, desert, temperate forest, tropical forest, savanna, snow field, tundra, volcanic)Fixed 1 · 4 · 7 · 10, one value per island — pick the one you want on the sailing screen
    Areas with a single island (levels 15 · 20 · 25 · 30)Goes up by 1 every 7 days (1 → 2 → … → 10 → 1)
    Safe House · Ancora · Tamed Island · training island · abandoned cities · event islandsAlways 1

    #Climate Resistance Level ✓ Live

    To sail to an island of Index 3 or higher you need the Climate Resistance Level for that island's climate (table above). If you are short, the sailing screen shows the requirement in red and the server refuses the departure (returning to your own land or the Safe House is not checked).

    How to raise it: just be on an island of that climate (while online) and it rises by itself — faster on higher-Index islands.

    Climates with their own resistanceTropical forest · desert · tundra · snow field · swamp · volcanic
    Temperate forest · grassland/savannaNo resistance of their own — your highest resistance of any kind is used

    💡 Tip

    The Safe House and Ancora are tropical-forest islands — whatever you are doing there, your tropical resistance keeps rising.

    ⚠️ Caution

    The highest resistance you can build right now is level 51 — so Index 9 (needs 62) and Index 10 (needs 80) islands cannot be sailed to yet.
