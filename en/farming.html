    Farming ✓ Live

    Learn the seed's skill, build a plot, sow, water it fully, fertilize for more harvest — when it matures the crop survives or withers depending on water and the island's climate

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Grow your own plants on plots you build. Crops grow in real time, even while you are offline. When the growing time is up, the crop either succeeds (ready to harvest) or withers — water it fully and pick crops that suit the island's climate to keep it alive. Harvesting gives the produce (food, flowers, fiber and more) plus seeds back.

    #What you can do ✓ Live

    • Build a Small Field / Large Field or a Flower Bed and plant one seed per plot
    • You can only plant seeds whose skill you have learned (Corn and Apple are available from the start) · trees and big plants need a large 2×2 plot
    • Give Water fully = high survival chance · Give Fertilizer = bigger harvest — neither one makes the crop grow faster
    • Place a Sprinkler to water many plots at once (multipurpose models also spread liquid fertilizer)
    • Use Rapid Growth to pay Warp Gems and finish a crop instantly
    • Harvesting gives Farming skill exp and levels up that seed's Farming Encyclopedia entry, which unlocks specializations

    #How to do it ✓ Live

    1. Build a plot (see Building). Plots you can plant in:
    PlotSize (tiles)Large seeds
    Small Field · Small Gray Field1×1No
    Large Field · Large Gray Field2×2Yes
    Small Flower Bed (Wood / Stone / Horn)1×1No
    Wide Flower Bed (Wood / Stone / Horn)2×2Yes
    1. Walk up to the plot, choose Plant Seed and pick a seed from your bag
    2. While it grows, use Give Water (with Water or Hot Spring Water — Sea Water does not work · fresh water in a container you filled works too, and the plot takes only what it still lacks) and Give Fertilizer (any item with the Compost property — see Fertilizer and yield)
    3. When it shows Available to Harvest, tap the plot — there is a harvest button named after the crop (done bare-handed, like the gathering menu); press it and wait for the progress bar to fill to get the produce · Remove Crop still works and gives the same produce
    4. The plot's label shows the crop's name (for example Wheat), not the raw seed code

    ⚠️ Caution

    Removing a crop before it is ripe gives the seed back (same level) but no produce and no exp.

    #Learn the skill before planting ✓ Live

    • Every character gets the Corn and Apple skills for free from the start
    • Every other seed needs its own skill in the Farming category first (unlocked at category level 10–60) — see the list in Skill list
    • Not learned yet = the game says «… skill is required» with a button to view the skill · the seed is kept
    • Large seeds (marked «*» in the growing-time table) can't go on a 1×1 plot — the seed is kept · a large crop already planted on a small plot keeps growing and can be harvested normally
    • 7 special seeds (Carnation · Watermelon · Cherry · Donarium Cherry Sapling · Peepkin · Spider Web Tree · Cacao Tree) have no skill to unlock — plant them as soon as you get them
    • Event seeds can only be planted during their event: Carnation · White Rose (1–31 May) · Jasmine (1–31 Aug) — outside it the game says it can't be planted · White Rose and Jasmine still need their normal skill

    #Halloween seeds ⏳ Not ready

    During the Halloween event (20 Oct – 10 Nov) every check-in gives 3 Peepkin Seeds and 1 Spider Web Tree Seed (the weekly Halloween ladder adds more) — they can only be planted during this window, on a large 2×2 plot, with no skill needed

    SeedGrows inProduce
    Peepkin Seed1 hourPeepkin
    Spider Web Tree Seed21 hoursSpider Web · Spider · Branch

    You can harvest after the event has ended and keep using the produce (event seeds can't be planted again until next year)

    #Key numbers

    #Growing time ✓ Live

    Growing times follow the original game's crop data — the 11 crops in the community sheet (Corn, Potato, Wheat, Sugar Cane, Rice, Tea Tree, Coffee, Grape, Fig, Banana, Watermelon) use the sheet values (at least 1 minute, no upper cap). Watering, fertilizing, sprinklers and rain do not make crops grow faster — the only ways to shorten it are the «Growth Time» specialization (−10%) and Rapid Growth.     #Water, climate and survival ✓ Live

    When the growing time is up, the game decides once whether the crop succeeds or withers (logging out and back in gives the same result). Crops planted before this system always succeed.

    survival chance = seed toughness × climate × water + Farming Ability bonus   (max 100%)
    • Seed toughness — 55–100% depending on the crop (Corn 90% · Banana 82% · Mango/Orange 70% · Apple 100%)
    • Water — based on the water in the plot when the time is up: full = full value · none at all = half · in between = proportional · 1 water item = 1 unit · crops need 1–40 units (Apple needs none · Corn 1 · Potato 2 · Wheat 3) · you can't water a full plot (no water is wasted) · rain waters plots too (Weather)
    • Climate — see the Climate Suitability line on the plot's info: Excellent ×1.05 · Good ×0.9 · Normal ×0.7 · Bad ×0.5 (fixed when you plant)
    • Farming Ability of whoever planted it (value at sowing) — each point adds 1% × toughness, up to 25 points · the Cultivation I–V skills give 4 points each · food and gear that raise Farming Ability count too
    • Fertilizer has no effect on survival · once the time is up you can no longer water or fertilize (the game says the crop is fully grown)

    Example, Corn (toughness 90%):

    ClimateFully wateredNever wateredFully watered + Cultivation I–V
    Excellent94.5%47.3%100%
    Good81%40.5%99%
    Normal63%31.5%81%
    Bad45%22.5%63%

    (Corn is a sheet crop, so it never goes below «Normal» — the «Bad» row applies to crops outside the sheet with the same 90% toughness, such as Cherry)

    A withered crop turns into a dead plant with only the Remove Crop button — removing it gives no produce, seed or exp (and costs no Energy). The message about it is in Thai.

    #Which climate each crop likes ✓ Live

    Climates sit in a row Snowfield – Tundra – Temperate – Grassland – Tropical – Desert – Volcano. An island matching the crop's favorite = Excellent · 1 step away = Good · 2 steps = Normal · 3 or more = Bad. Swamp islands count as Temperate · Savanna islands and the tamed islands count as Grassland.

    The 11 crops in the sheet (Corn, Potato, Wheat, Sugar Cane, Rice, Tea Tree, Coffee, Grape, Fig, Banana, Watermelon) follow the sheet's rule: the favorite island = Excellent · the sheet's worst island = Bad (Rice, Banana, Coffee — Tundra · Tea Tree — Tropical) · other islands are counted by distance from the favorite but never below Normal

    #Fertilizer and yield ✓ Live

    • Fertilize until Soil Fertility is 100% = the harvest rises to the crop's «fully fertilized» amount in its table · half = halfway between «none» and «full» · extra fertilizer adds nothing · the fraction is a chance for one more piece
    • Crops outside the 11 sheet crops use the old rule: fully fertilized ×1.6 · half ×1.3 (for example fully fertilized Corn used to give 1.6× the base)
    • Fertilizer counts by the amount of each piece (the same number the fertilizer window shows) · crops need 10–200 depending on the type (Corn 10 · Potato 30 · Wheat 40) · the fertilize option disappears once it is full
    • The Nutritious attribute on a fertilizer adds +12.5% amount per level
    • … Harvesting Buff attributes (Vegetable · Fruit · Grain · Flower · Leaf · Wood · Fiber) add +10% (level 1) up to +50% (level 11) harvest for crops of that type · with several pieces, the strongest one counts
    • Making yield-boosting fertilizer yourself — the «Fermented Fruit Growth Fertilizer» recipe gives only one buff, chosen by the additive: Charcoal → Vegetable · Seashell or Horseshoe Crab Shell → Fruit · Bone → Grain · the «Fermented Leaf/Stalk Growth Fertilizer» recipe: seeds (grain seeds too) → Fiber · fish → Wood · fruit → Leaf · grain → Flower (fertilizer made before the update keeps buffing every type)

    #Sprinklers ✓ Live

    Touch a sprinkler and choose Give Water — every plot in its spray area that is still growing gets water by model, with no water taken from your bag. A sprinkler has limited uses that recharge one at a time (the remaining uses are on its info page · when it's out, the game tells you how many minutes until the next charge).

    #Rapid Growth ✓ Live

    A crop that is still growing has a Rapid Growth button — pay Warp Gems and it finishes instantly (your character's real Warp Gems are spent, the same number the window shows). The price drops as the remaining time goes down. Success or withering is decided right away with the same chance (Rapid Growth does not help it survive). Anyone allowed to use the plot can press it (and pays themselves).

    Time leftPrice (Warp Gems)
    1 minute or less1
    35 minutes (Lv1 Corn just planted)17
    1 hour30
    9 hours155
    24 hours390

    Between these points the price moves in a straight line.

    #Farming Encyclopedia ✓ Live

    Each seed type has its own encyclopedia level (max 20). The encyclopedia page shows the real growing time, water and fertilizer of the 11 sheet crops (you see them after returning to the title screen or logging in again). Each harvest gives +20 exp, and the number of harvests needed depends on the crop (fast crops need more harvests than trees). Exp you already had is kept and your level is recalculated with these thresholds (nobody loses a level).

    #Plots owned by others ✓ Live

    Whoever built a plot can always do everything with it · others can sow, water, fertilize, use Rapid Growth or uproot/harvest only when:

    Where the plot isWhat others need
    On someone's Domainall 3 permissions Use + Give + Take from the Domain owner (friends use the Friends tab), or that plot's own per-item permission
    Outside any Domaina per-item permission the plot owner opened for that plot — otherwise only the plot owner can use it

    No permission = the plot's buttons are greyed out and nothing is taken from your bag · a sprinkler on someone else's Domain needs the «Use» permission · encyclopedia exp only goes to whoever planted the crop — harvesting a friend's plot gives the produce but no encyclopedia exp · see Domain for setting permissions · boxes and workbenches on someone else's Domain use a different set of permissions, see Boxes, workbenches and pens on someone else's Domain

    #Tips

    💡 Tip

    Fill the water before the crop matures — the water in the plot at that moment is what decides survival. Plot water never drains by itself, so one full watering is enough, and rain can do it for you.

    💡 Tip

    On a tamed island (Grassland), Corn, Onion, Flax and Bean get an «Excellent» climate. To grow tundra or desert crops with better survival, learn Cultivation I–V first.

    💡 Tip

    Harvested vegetables, fruit and mushrooms spoil within 3 days, while grains (corn, rice, wheat, beans, barley) and apples don't — see Preserving food.

    💡 Tip

    Event seeds (Carnation, White Rose, Jasmine, Peepkin, Spider Web Tree) can only be planted during their event; outside it the game tells you the seed can't be planted.

    #Some produce is still a generic item ✓ Live

    For 21 crops the original game's specific produce is unknown, so they currently give a generic item instead, e.g. ornamental flowers → Flower · Maple Tree/Ginkgo Tree → Leaf · Banana Tree, Mango, Orange → Tropical Fruit · Fig Tree → Normal Fruit. Some of them (e.g. Banana, Fig, Lavender, Purple Tulip) carry a plant name tag, so recipes asking for that plant still accept them. This may change once more data is found.
