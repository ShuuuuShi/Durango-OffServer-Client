    Skill list ✓ Live

    Every skill and every tier across all 13 categories — required category level, points, effect, and the recipes/blueprints/animals each tier unlocks

    Every skill in the game, by category and group — how to learn/rewind skills, skill points and research are on Skills and Careers · effects on your stats are on Character stats

    • Category Lv = the skill category level you need before learning that tier · earlier tiers must be learned first
    • Points = skill points spent (you get 10 + 5 per character level — 305 at Lv60, not enough for everything, so pick your paths)
    • Effect / unlocks = the in-game description + the crafting recipes, blueprints, gatherable items, combat moves or tameable animals that tier unlocks

    ℹ️ Note

    Effect descriptions are the in-game text — some percentage effects (e.g. weapon critical damage) may not be calculated on this server yet · everything under Unlocks (recipes, blueprints, gatherables, tameable animals) really works
