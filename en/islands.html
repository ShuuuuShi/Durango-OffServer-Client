    Islands & Travel ✓ Live

    Island types (Ancora, Safe House, Tamed Island, Unstable Islands, Civilized Islands, Lawless Islands) · Unstable Island lifespan · private Tamed Islands for Supporters · sailing, balloons, returning to a Stable Island · island levels, Unstable Index and danger

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    The world of Durango is an archipelago. Every island has its own level and climate. Some islands are safe and permanent (you can settle there), others are Unstable Islands — more dangerous, with better loot, and only around for a limited time. Everyone on the server shares the same world: you meet, help and compete with other players on the same islands.

    #What you can do

    • Start on Ancora → build a raft → arrive at The Company's Safe House
    • Take the Hot Air Balloon to claim your own Tamed Island, then declare a Domain · players with the Supporter role get a private Tamed Island and choose who may enter it
    • Sail from a Harbor to Unstable Islands in 8 climates, level 15–60, to gather and hunt — an Unstable Island lasts 12 hours to 3 days, then disappears and a new one takes its place
    • Pick your own difficulty with each island's Unstable Index (1–10)
    • At level 56+, sail to the Lawless Islands where players can fight each other — Lawless Islands (PvP)
    • Go home for free at any time with Sail to the last Stable Island or the warp buttons on Warps & Rifts

    #Ancora (starting island) ✓ Live

    Every new character starts on Ancora — the tutorial island, rebuilt from the original game's real map. Follow the tutorial, finish the raft (4 logs · 6 reed stems) and it carries you to the Safe House.

    • Resources were rebuilt to match the original: everything is on ground you can walk to — nothing on cliffs or unreachable plateaus · the tutorial spots have what the tutorial asks for (dates at the train cars · reeds at the stream · stones by the lake · branches and stones at the bonfire · log trees and reeds around the small lake by the raft) — details in Getting Started
    • Resource amounts and regrow speed are the same as on other islands (no special multiplier anymore)
    • Resting at the bonfire on Ancora empties your fatigue in 5 seconds
    • Animals: the Brachiosaurus stay in the lake only · 3 Compsognathus herds near the spawn valley and along the stream — harmless, they run when hit, a good first hunt (Compsognathus on Ancora and the Safe House) · the 2 at the train cars are scenery models
    • You can sail back to visit Ancora from a Harbor, but you can only leave by raft — you must build it again every time

    #The Company's Safe House ✓ Live

    The hub for new players — K and the Company's staff, a workbench, a campfire, a notice board, a Harbor, a balloon station and Warp Holes you can use right away without finding them. If you haven't set a Waypoint anywhere, the Safe House is your "home". Harmless Compsognathus herds roam around the arrival point.

    #Tamed Island ✓ Live

    A level 10 grassland island for settling down — declare a Domain, build a house, farm, raise animals. Expanding a Domain on a Tamed Island is free, and there is no climate fatigue from the island itself.

    Tamed Islands have no wild animals at all — no dinosaurs or bosses spawn there, so it's a safe island for building and farming · your own pets and caged animals are unaffected · to hunt or tame, go to another island.

    Tamed Islands are a shared world — everyone who picks the same island layout lives on the same island. You see each other and can visit each other's homes, like a village where everyone stakes out their own plot. Spots already declared show as owned, so look for the next free one. (Supporters can use a private Tamed Island instead — see below.)

    Your first trip to your Tamed Island is by Hot Air Balloon from the Safe House (see Hot Air Balloon below). You can pick from 5 island layouts (next section).

    #Choosing your Tamed Island layout ✓ Live

    The first time you press Leave at the balloon station, the Declare Tamed Island screen lets you pick from 5 grassland island layouts, each with a different map.

    • You choose once — the layout can't be changed afterwards
    • Players who already have a Tamed Island keep it and are not moved
    • Each layout is its own shared world — players who pick the same layout live together
    • Same rules for all: level 10 · Index 1 · no wild animals · free Domain expansion

    #Private Tamed Island (Supporter) ✓ Live

    Players with the Supporter role get a private Tamed Island — the same layout you picked, but for that character only. Other players can't see it or enter it (except people you allow — next section). It's still the same server as everyone else: chat, friends, clans and the market work as usual.

    • One per character · same island rules as the shared Tamed Island (level 10 · no wild animals · free Domain expansion)
    • Switch by typing in chat: /tamed private = your private island (the default) · /tamed shared = the shared Tamed Island · typing it while on the Tamed Island moves you right away · anywhere else, it applies next time you go to your Tamed Island
    • Everything you built on the shared Tamed Island stays where it is — the private island starts empty (you can move your old land over once — see "Moving your land to your private island")
    • The role is checked every time you log in — if it ends, you go to the shared Tamed Island next time and your private island is kept; get the role back and you return
    • Everyone else keeps using the shared Tamed Island as before

    #Visiting private Tamed Islands ✓ Live

    The island owner (a Supporter) decides who may enter in the Island Entry Permissions window (the Domain screen on the private island) — tick Clan Member · Friend · Outsider, several at once if you like ("Outsider" = everyone)

    • Default = all closed, only the owner can enter
    • Closing a group sends that group's visitors back to their own Tamed Island at once
    • Up to 20 visitors at a time (the owner not counted) · allowed players can visit even while the owner is offline · an online owner gets a message when someone arrives
    • Removing a visitor: tap them on your island → the kick-from-island button → they're sent back to their own Tamed Island · Block them = sent back at once and can't enter your island again

    To visit — use the sailing menu at a Harbor (Random Sailing) · you arrive at the island's arrival point

    • On someone else's island: walk, chat and gather natural resources · inside the owner's land the owner's permissions apply (outsiders normally can't build or open boxes/cages) · you can't declare land or build outside the owner's land
    • Only Supporters' private Tamed Islands can be visited this way (the shared Tamed Island is open to everyone anyway)

    #Moving your land to your private island ✓ Live

    A Supporter can move their land from the shared Tamed Island to their private island once per character (no deadline) — houses, buildings, boxes with everything inside, cages and the animals in them are placed at the exact same spot on the private island.

    1. Type /tamed move in chat → you get a summary of what will move
    2. Type /tamed move confirm within 60 seconds to confirm (/tamed move cancel = cancel)
    • Works while your private island has no land yet · if something on the private island is in the way, you're told to remove it first · a failed move, or no land on the shared island, costs nothing — your move stays available and everything stays put
    • Things your friends built on your land move with it · other players' animals in your cages go back to their owners · anything outside your land stays where it is
    • Confirming while standing on the shared Tamed Island takes you to your private island right away · afterwards the Tamed Island button takes you to the private island

    #Island lifespan ✓ Live

    Unstable Islands and event islands last 12 hours to 3 days depending on the island, and they don't all expire at the same time. The time left is shown as Island Life Span on the map.

    WhenWhat happens
    1 hour and 10 minutes leftMessage "Island will disappear after …"
    Expired"The island will disappear soon. Returning to your Waypoint in …" — a 60-second countdown, then you're taken to your Waypoint (home island · none = the Safe House) · if you're dead, you're revived for free first
    About 1.5 minutes after expiryA fresh island takes its place — you can't sail to it until then
    • Anything left on the island disappears with it: buildings, houses, boxes and their contents, camps, land claims, dropped items — except pets (they stay with their owner) and vehicles (moved to the Safe House)
    • The new island: resources back in full · all animals respawn · crater caches re-rolled · single islands go up one Index · the island's shape stays the same
    • A newly spawned island shows a NEW badge on the sailing screen for its first hour
    • Maps you bought/opened, fog, explored markers and the full-exploration reward start over on the new island (Map and exploration) · the island's message board is cleared
    • If you logged out there and the island expires, you'll be at your Waypoint next time you log in, with "The island disappeared and you were moved to your Waypoint."
    • No lifespan: Ancora · Safe House · Tamed Islands · training island · Civilized Islands · Lawless Islands

    #Training island (tropical) ✓ Live

    A level 18 tropical island with the survival tutorial (find a crater, find the harbor). You can sail there from any Harbor. Its Index is always 1, it has no predators, and island fatigue is halved.

    #Civilized Islands ✓ Live

    Four abandoned cities at level 40 (grassland · temperate · tropical · tundra) — stable islands where you can declare a Domain. Island fatigue is halved.

    #Event islands ✓ Live

    Islands from the original game's past events (grassland festival · Summer Club · Season 1/2 islands) are open as normal islands with Index 1 and a lifespan like Unstable Islands. If you log out while on an event island, you'll wake up at home next time (see "Logging out on a temporary island" below).

    #Lawless Islands ✓ Live

    Five level 60 islands (volcano · swamp · snowfield · temperate · tropical) where players can fight each other — open from level 56 · Index 1 · no lifespan · no Domains · details on Lawless Islands (PvP)

    #Travelling between islands

    #Sailing from a Harbor ✓ Live

    1. Walk to a Harbor and tap it → choose Sail
    2. The sea map opens — pick a climate/level, then an island by the Index you want
    3. Confirm "Do you want to move to the … island?" → you land at that island's arrival point
    • Sailing is free — no T-Stones — and islands are not level-locked. You can go to a level 60 island at level 1, but you'll tire very fast · except the Lawless Islands, which need level 56+
    • Islands with Index 3 or higher need enough climate resistance (table below), or you can't set sail
    • An island that is respawning (about 1.5 minutes after it expires) can't be sailed to
    • Your pets and everything in your bag come with you

    #Random Sailing ✓ Live

    The Harbor's sailing menu has 2 buttons for visiting other players' private Tamed Islands (Visiting private Tamed Islands)

    • To Tamed Island → search a player by name → pick who to visit
    • Random Sailing → go to someone's island that is open to Outsiders and not full · none open = you get a message

    Sailing there is free · not allowed / full = you can't go, and a message says why

    #Returning to a Stable Island ✓ Live

    At the Harbor of an Unstable Island you get Sail to the last Stable Island and "Return to the Tamed Island harbor you started from?" — both take you straight to your home island, for free.

    • Home island = the island where you used Designate as Waypoint (your own bed/tent) · never set = the Safe House
    • Leaving any island clears that island's automatic checkpoint (a bed/tent Waypoint is kept)

    #Hot Air Balloon ✓ Live

    The balloon station at the Safe House offers two things:

    • Leave — go to your Tamed Island (the first time, the Declare Tamed Island screen opens first so you can pick 1 of 5 layouts)
    • Board Hot Air Balloon — fly freely over the island, even across water. Free to board · flights last up to 15 minutes, then you land automatically · you can't land on water/cliffs — you'll be sent back to the station

    • Single islands at level 15/20/25/30 move up one Index each time the island expires and respawns (1 → 10, then back to 1) — it never changes during an island's lifespan
    • Index 2+ adds fatigue: Index 2 = +1.0/min · 8 = +4.0/min · 10 = +5.0/min

    #Animals & safe zones ✓ Live

    • Every wild animal (bosses and guards included) spawns at island level ±5, except on high-level islands: level 55 islands = random 60–70 · level 60 islands = random 88–99 (Lawless Islands included) — see Islands of level 55 and up
    • Tamed Islands have no wild animals
    • A 12-tile radius around every Harbor and arrival point is a safe zone: animals don't spawn or walk in there, and predators chasing you give up when you run into it · but if you hit an animal first, it can hit back from the edge while you are within its reach (When an animal is hit) · the zone only keeps out animals — on a Lawless Island players can still hit you inside it

    #Logging out on a temporary island ✓ Live

    If you log out or disconnect while on a temporary island — an Unstable Island with Index 2+, an event island, or the training island — the next time you log in you'll be on your home island (or the Safe House) with the message "The island disappeared and you were moved to your Waypoint." Everything you carry and your pets come with you.

    • Stable islands (Safe House · Ancora · Tamed Island · Civilized Islands · Lawless Islands · Unstable Islands with Index 1) → you come back where you were
    • Arriving on a temporary island by boat doesn't move you — it only happens on the next login after you log out
    • An island that expires while you're offline → you're always moved to your Waypoint, even at Index 1 (Island lifespan)

    #Permanent crater caches ✓ Live

    On Unstable Islands with Index 2 or higher, every crater becomes a resource cache — 10 natural resources of one kind around the crater (5-tile radius), guarded by 3 animals that are ×1.5 tougher than normal (on top of the island's Index). The cache type depends on the island's climate, e.g. iron mine, rubber trees, fir forest · cache types and guards are re-rolled each time the island respawns.

    #Tips

    💡 Tip

    Set a Waypoint (bed/tent) on the island you want as home before heading out — returning to a Stable Island and waking up after logging out on a temporary island both bring you back there.

    💡 Tip

    Beginners should start with island 1 of each climate (Index 1) — no resistance needed and animals aren't boosted.

    ⚠️ Caution

    Don't build a home or store things on Unstable or event islands — when the island expires, everything goes with it · before logging out on an Unstable Island with Index 2+, pick up anything you placed there — next time you log in you won't be on that island anymore.
