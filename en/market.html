    Market and Shop ✓ Live

    Trade with other players at the Market for T-Stones and buy from the Shop with T-Stones — this server has a single currency and no real-money top-ups.

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    The main currency on this server is T-Stones. You use them to trade with other players at the Market, buy things in the Shop, pay for cargo, create a clan, and more. This server has no real-money top-ups — everything is bought with T-Stones earned in the game.

    #What you can do

    • List items from your bag at the Market at your own price
    • Search/filter listings and buy what others are selling
    • Star listings you like to keep them in Favorites
    • Collect money from sales · take back items that didn't sell
    • Buy packages, random boxes, items and tickets in the Shop

    #Currency ✓ Live

    This server uses a single wallet: T-Stones.

    CurrencyUsed for
    T-StonesMain money — Market, Shop, cargo fees, clan creation, Clan Funds, etc. · you can hold up to 99,999,999
    LoyaltySeparate points given as a bonus by some Shop items — spent in the Loyalty Shop
    TicketsSome things are tickets (e.g. map-opening tickets), kept separately in your wallet

    Some Shop items show their price with a Gem/Coin icon — on this server they cost the same number of T-Stones (1 to 1), and the balance shown under the Gem/Coin icons is your T-Stone balance.

    Where T-Stones come from: discovering new places on the map and full-exploration rewards · Quests · daily attendance · mini-games · Warp · gift mail (Mail) · selling at the Market

    #Market: selling ✓ Live

    1. Open the Market menu → Sell tab
    2. Pick items from your bag (equipped or locked items can't be listed)
    3. Set the price per item and how long to sell → confirm — the items leave your bag right away
    4. While on sale you can press Cancel Listing at any time; the item returns to your bag
    • You can pick several items at once; each is listed separately at the same price
    • You sell the actual item — the buyer gets its durability, color and tags as they are
    • Food on the Market does not spoil while listed (see Preserving)

    #Market: searching and buying ✓ Live

    • Search by name, category, tag, price range and level range; sort by price, time, level or durability
    • Many screens have a shortcut to search the Market, e.g. material slots when building/repairing
    • Press Buy → pay T-Stones, the item goes straight into your bag · you can't buy your own listings
    • Star listings to keep them in Favorites — the server remembers them after you log out

    #Market: collecting money and unsold items ✓ Live

    • When something sells you get a notice if you're online → go to the Sold tab and press Receive per item or for all — the money is not added until you collect it
    • If the listing time runs out with no buyer → the item moves to the Expired tab; press Collect to take it back
    • If you don't take it back in time, the server mails the item back to you (it is not lost)

    #Shop ✓ Live

    Open the Shop menu — tabs Package · Animals · Item · House · Express Cargo · Loyalty Shop · it sells packages, random boxes, items and tickets, about 118 in total.

    1. Pick an item → Buy → T-Stones are taken (or Loyalty points in the Loyalty Shop)
    2. Most things wait in the Shop's Storage tab → press Receive per item or Receive All · some go straight into your bag
    3. Some items have a purchase limit or a character-level requirement
    ItemValue
    Gem/Coin prices showncharged as T-Stones, 1 to 1
    Unclaimed items in Storageexpire after 30 days
    Items waiting in Storageup to 100

    ℹ️ Note

    There is no top-up page — the + buttons next to the Gem/Coin icons are still there, but pressing them only says there is nothing to buy · packs that give T-Stones are not sold on this server

    💡 Tip

    • Before setting a price, search the same item to see what others ask
    • After a sale, remember to collect your money in the Sold tab — it is not added automatically
    • Items in the Shop's Storage can expire; claim them soon
