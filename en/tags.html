    Item attributes ✓ Live

    Latent and rare (green) attributes — how you get them while gathering, how they carry over when crafting, and what each one does

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Each material may come with attributes such as «Firm», «High Density», or green ones like «Black Sheen». When you craft, the attributes of the material in the main slot become attributes of the product — more attack, more durability, or special effects like bleeding.

    #Where to see attributes

    1. Open Item Info for the item
    2. Attributes show as chips with their level — the color tells how rare they are:
    ColorMeaning
    GreenRare Attribute — a special effect
    Yellowa good attribute
    Normala basic attribute
    Graya negative attribute
    1. Tap a chip to read its description · combat/gathering rare attributes have no number on the stats page — read the chip's description instead

    #Attributes from gathering ✓ Live

    When you gather, butcher or harvest a farm plot, each piece can get attributes in two ways:

    • Source attributes — some sources always give fixed attributes (for example Mammoth leather is Oily) · the first (main) one gets +1 level for each point of the island's Unstable Index above 1 — the Index counts up to 6 on islands below level 60 and up to 8 on level 60 islands · an Index of 6 or higher only counts fully on a Flawless gather; otherwise it counts as Index 5
    • Random latent attributes — yellow · gray · green are rolled separately, so one piece can get several colors at once, with no limit on the number · none of this depends on your skills or the island's Index
    ColorChance per pieceLevel
    Yellow (good)12.5% · farm plot products 50%4
    Gray (negative)about 5.4% (3/7 of the yellow chance) · only for materials that have a gray pair5
    Green (rare)bare hands 0.1% · holding a Lv30 tool 0.2% · Lv60 0.3% · butchering a big animal ×21
    • Multipliers — the «Secrets of Gathering» attribute on the tool you are holding (worn gear doesn't count), and titles/clan research with «Latent/Rare Attribute Rate», multiply the chance of all 3 colors by (1 + the total) · up to 100% per color
    • Farm plots — the Lv20 encyclopedia mastery multiplies only the color you choose ×1.2 (yellow 50% → 60%, or green 0.1% → 0.12%) · green from farms exists only for flower, fruit, grain and other edible products · edible vegetables, mushrooms, sugarcane, coffee and so on roll 1 of the 3 food greens below · leaves, tea leaves and spider web have no green
    • Bonus pieces (from «Extra Extra» or gathering skills) get attributes just like the main piece

    #Flawless Success effects when gathering ✓ Live

    On a Flawless gather, butcher or harvest, some materials get special effects (for the chance of a Flawless result see Success and failure):

    MaterialEffect on a Flawless result
    Ordinary leather+1 processing chance · gets «High Durability» 1 and «Flexibility» 1
    Pelt (furred hide)gets «High Durability» 1 (no extra processing chance)
    Armor leather+3 processing chances · gets «High Durability» 1 and «Flexibility» 3
    Ore+1 processing chance · gets «Flexibility» 1 · becomes a «Big Chunk» instead of a «Chunk»
    Cotton from farmsgets «High Durability» 7
    Flax from farms (tropical flax too)gets «High Durability» 7 and «Tough» 7
    • Starting processing chances of materials: ore 5 · ordinary leather 4 · everything else 3 (pelt and armor leather 3) — so Flawless ore has 6, ordinary leather 5 and armor leather 6 (more in Crafting)
    • Made a mistake while gathering (you still get the item — see Success and failure) gets «Low Durability» level 2, and ore, ordinary leather and armor leather lose 1 processing chance (ore falls to 4 · ordinary leather to 3 · armor leather to 2)
    • Flawless no longer boosts the durability of gathered items, and no longer adds levels to yellow attributes
    • A Flawless craft also gives the product an extra processing chance — see Crafting

    #Attributes carried over when crafting ✓ Live

    • Only the material in the main slot passes attributes on
    • Nothing is rolled away any more — at every crafting step (including steps whose product is a part: blade, string, plank …) every attribute the product type accepts carries over, and there is no «attributes were lost» message · attributes can only be missing in 3 cases: the average is below 1 · they weren't in the main slot · the product type doesn't accept that attribute
    • Several pieces in the main slot:
      • Yellow/gray/normal attributes — the product's level = the sum of the levels over all pieces in the main slot ÷ the number of pieces, rounded down (a piece without that attribute counts as 0) · a result of 0 = not carried over
      • Green attributes — one piece in the main slot is enough (no averaging) · the same green attribute never stacks
    • Titles and «Secrets of Gathering» have no effect when crafting (they only work when gathering)
    • Processing recipes (trimming, smelting, tanning and so on) keep every attribute · a Flawless processing gives the attributes the recipe adds +1 level · a processing mistake gives 1 level less on the attributes the recipe adds (never below the item's own level)
    • There is no limit on how many attributes can carry over
    • The Expected Result window for crafting/building shows the attributes the product will have and their levels before you start — yellow and gray match the real result, while rare attributes that are picked 1-of-2 when the craft finishes show as «???»

    Example: the main slot of boots takes 3 pieces of leather — the first has «High Density» level 6, the other two have none → the product gets level (6+0+0) ÷ 3 = 2 · if all three are level 6 the product gets the full 6 · a single piece of leather with «Black Sheen» (green) is enough for the product to get the green attribute

    Clothes / accessoriesclothes, hats, gloves, shoes, bags, accessories

    #Attribute levels on gear ✓ Live

    Attributes that give stats — every weather resistance, Defense Buff, Attack Buff, accuracy, critical, Durability Buff, crafting/gathering skills, base stats, stealth, Storage Capacity and so on — can come two ways on the same item:

    • From the item's design — many grow with the item's level, e.g. Winter Fur Gloves carry Cold Resistance Buff = 1 + level × 0.05 (Lv60 = 4) · fractions round half up (3.95 → 4), except Storage Capacity, which rounds down
    • Latent attributes picked up while gathering/crafting

    The level that really counts = design + latent (up to that attribute's maximum level) · the item info screen shows the total · the success label after crafting/gathering shows «Lv design → Lv total»

    Example (1 resistance point = −3% fatigue of that kind)Cold ResistanceCold fatigue
    Winter Fur Gloves Lv60, no latent4−12%
    Same gloves + latent Cold Resistance Buff 26−18%
    Full 5-piece winter set Lv60, no latent24−72%
    • Material/structure attributes (Strong, High Density and the like that recipes check) work as before: the latent value replaces the design value · recipe requirements do not change
    • When crafting, only the latent value carries over; the result then adds its own design value
    • Storage Capacity (from the bag-pocket modification or latent): level = extra bag slots (Storage)
    • An item at zero durability — none of its attributes work (resistance, immunity, capacity, buffs) until repaired · the stats window updates as soon as it breaks or is repaired
    • Food attributes in an item's design (Poisonous to Humans · Cold · Stamina Food …) are also worked out from the item's level — see the effects when eaten in Eating and drinking

    #Tips

    💡 Tip

    Green attributes are very rare (0.1–0.3% per piece) — hold a high-level tool with «Secrets of Gathering» (×2) and butcher big animals (×2) to see them more often · a high Unstable Index raises the level of a source's fixed attribute, not the green chance

    💡 Tip

    Several pieces in the main slot average the attribute level (rounded down) — fill every slot with pieces that share the attribute · for green attributes a single piece is enough

    💡 Tip

    Long crafting chains no longer make attributes disappear — but some product types don't accept some attributes, so check the table above to see what each attribute is used for

    #Not on the server yet: hazards these attributes block ⏳ Not ready

    Some gear has «Lava Damage Prevention», «Spark Immunity» or «T-Rex Roar Prevention» — wearing it shows the status icon, but the server has no damaging lava, sparks or roars yet, so there is no visible effect. «Volcanic Storm Prevention», on the other hand, now works fully — see Volcanic Storm
