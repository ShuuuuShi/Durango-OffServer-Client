    Survival ✓ Live

    Life, Health, Energy and Fatigue gauges · resting, drinking and washing · body temperature and getting too cold or too hot

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Every island in Durango wears you down — fatigue piles up from work and climate, walking burns Energy, and your body temperature follows the weather. This page explains the gauges on screen, how to recover, and the server's real numbers.

    #What you can do

    • Rest next to a shelter or bonfire to lower Fatigue and recover Life
    • Drink Water and Wash at the water's edge to ease heat fatigue
    • Draw Water into a container for later
    • Eat to refill Energy (details in Eating)
    • Get out of the rain, warm up at a fire, eat hot/cold food to control your body temperature
    • Log out to rest — time offline counts as resting

    The on-screen fatigue icons, from fine to exhausted

    #Fatigue ✓ Live

    LevelEffect
    60 and upTired — a warning that you should rest
    90 and upExhausted — you cannot gather, craft or fight; a message tells you to rest or go back to an island with a milder climate, until it drops below 90

    Where Fatigue comes from (per minute · the screen lists it by category)

    1. Island level vs. your level — on unstable islands: (0.04 + 0.001 × your level) × 60 × a factor, where factor = 1 − 0.05 × (your level − island level), never below 0.5 · abandoned-city islands and the training island: half · Safe House, Ancora, Tamed Island = 0
    2. The island's climate — 0.5 × (0.35 × island level) per minute (minimum 0.5) on islands that have a climate
    3. Current weather — rain, storms, snow, volcanic ash (table in Weather)
    4. The island's Unstable Index — Index 2 and up adds Index ÷ 2 per minute (Weather)
    5. Work — gathering, building, repairing and so on (Gathering)

    #Resting ✓ Live

    You can rest while standing on or next to a shelter: a player-built tent/house/bed/bonfire, an island's fixed campfire, or the island's landing point · walking ends the rest

    Rest typeFatigueLifeHealth
    Normal rest−9.1/min+30/min (+90 counting natural regen)+12/min
    Resting on Ancora×3 faster×3×3
    The tutorial bonfire on AncoraAlmost instant——
    Offline (logged out)−9.1/min+30/min—
    • Resting at a bonfire gives an EXP ×2 buff for 30 minutes (it keeps counting after you stand up) and warms you
    • Resting refills the combat exhaustion gauge (Combat)
    • Shelters inside a house with a complete bedroom/living-room set give extra buffs (Building)
    • Offline time counts as resting for up to 8 hours (you must be away more than 1 minute) — 100 Fatigue is gone after about 11 minutes offline

    💡 Tip

    A system message tells you how many Fatigue points per minute you are losing — use it to compare shelters.

    #Water: drink, wash, draw ✓ Live

    Stand at the water's edge and the buttons appear.

    ButtonWhereTimeResult
    Drink WaterWater's edge5 secondsEnergy +5 · Water status 5 min
    WashIn water, or at a finished bathtub/spa5 secondsFatigue −5 · Washed 6 min · Wet 2 min
    Draw WaterWater's edge, with a container4 seconds · 2 EnergyRiver/lake = fresh water · sea = seawater
    • Drinking from a water source does not count toward fullness — drink as often as you like
    • Drinking seawater gives Thirst — use it for cooking/preserving instead (Preserving)

    #Timed actions one at a time ✓ Live

    • Drinking, washing, drawing water and every other action with a progress bar (gathering, butchering, dyeing, taming, Look Around, crafting …) run one at a time
    • Starting a new one before the current bar is full cancels the old one, which gives nothing · mashing the button only gives the last one
    • Drinking and washing apply when the bar is full · washing at a bathtub needs you to still be next to it when the bar fills
    • Durations and effects (fatigue removed, energy) are unchanged · the energy for drawing water is paid at the start and not refunded if cancelled

    #Fullness ✓ Live

    Every food adds fullness (there is no gauge on screen). At 100 you get the Full status and cannot eat more · fullness drains from full to empty in 30 minutes — details in Eating.

    #Body temperature ✓ Live

    There is no temperature gauge on screen — watch your character's pose (shivering when cold, a heat pose when hot) and your status icons.

    ItemValue
    Normal37°
    Rate of change0.6° per minute toward the target temperature
    Cold pose / hot pose≤ 36.4° / ≥ 37.6°
    Hypothermia≤ 35° (clears once you warm up to 36.2°)
    Heatstroke≥ 39° (clears once you cool down to 38°)
    While sickHealth −24/min · Fatigue +50%

    Target temperature = 37 + island climate + current weather + statuses

    Island climate±Weather±Status±
    Heat (tropical forest)+1Cloudy−0.2Wet−1.5
    Scorching Sun (desert)+2Rain−0.6Bonfire+2.5
    Volcanic heat+2Heavy rain−1.2Hot Food+0.6
    Humid (swamp)+0.5Snow−1Cold Food−0.6
    Chill (tundra)−1.5Heavy snow−2
    Strong Winds (snow field)−1Volcanic ash / Volcanic Storm+0.5 / +1.5
    • Resting under a roof: climate + weather count only 35%
    • Standing in rain, heavy rain or heavy snow keeps you Wet (except while resting in a shelter or at a fire)

    ⚠️ Caution

    A desert or volcano in full sun puts your target at exactly 39° — heatstroke in about 3 minutes · wash (Wet −1.5), eat cold food or rest under a roof. A snow field in heavy snow while Wet targets 32.5° — hypothermia in about 3 minutes; get to a fire fast.
