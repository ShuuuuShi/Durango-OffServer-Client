    Preserving food and spoilage ✓ Live

    Fresh food fully spoils in 3 days — smoke, dry, pickle or can it to make it last longer · each item can be processed 3 times

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Meat, fish, vegetables and fruit slowly spoil in real time. Preservation recipes make food last longer, at the cost of losing the ingredient's status/buff effects. On top of that, every item has limited processing chances — it can be cooked or preserved at most 3 times.

    #What you can do

    • Smoke, dry, pickle and can fresh food so it keeps longer
    • Draw sea water to make Salt for pickling
    • List food on the market without worrying about spoilage (listed items stop spoiling)

    #Preservation recipes

    All of them are in the Cooking category, Preservation group · put an edible item in the main slot.

    #What preservation removes

    • The Raw property is removed by every recipe · Freshness is removed by smoking, drying and pickling
    • Ingredient properties that give statuses or buffs are stripped, e.g. Hot, Cold, Recovery Medicine, Attack Buff, Defense Buff and other medicine/buff effects
    • Taste and energy properties (e.g. Boost Umami, Boost Sweetness, Deep Fried) are kept
    • Harmful properties such as Poisonous to Humans are not removed (preserving doesn't make food safe)
    • The food type's own built-in effects (e.g. Bread's max-energy buff) stay, since it is still the same item
    • Sausage, Premium Sausage and Witch Sausage also don't inherit status/buff properties from the meat used

    #Spoilage

    #What spoils

    SpoilsDoesn't spoil
    Meat, intestine, fish, eggs, fruit, vegetables, mushrooms, berries (47 kinds), including the same items after cooking or preserving, e.g. grilled meat, pickled meatNew dishes (bread, soup, steak, sausage, cheese, sashimi, etc.) · drinks, milk, water, sea water · Apple · grains · flowers, leaves, roots, herbs · insects · seeds · nuts

    #How fast

    Fresh food starts at Durability 100 and drops steadily to 0 in 3 days (33.3 per day). The speed is the same for every item, so preserved food with a higher max durability lasts longer.

    StateMax durabilityFully spoiled after
    Fresh1003 days
    Dried · Pickled · Improvised Canned1504.5 days
    Smoked165about 5 days
    Standard Canned2006 days
    • Durability counts down on screen by itself
    • Food spoils everywhere — bag, warehouse, boxes, pet bags, mail, entrust tables — except items listed on the market (paused, then resumes when bought or withdrawn)
    • Preserving partly spoiled food = the remaining durability + the extra (e.g. 50/100 → dried becomes 100/150)

    #When it's fully spoiled

    • The item does not disappear and you can still eat it — energy, taste and satiety work as normal
    • But eating it gives Stomach Pain, and the food's status/buff effects don't work
    • The game shows it as Destroyed (sorted to the end of the bag) and it can't go into crafting slots · the in-game text says to Repair it, but food can't be repaired

    #Sea water and salt

    1. Stand by the sea and choose Draw Water → you get Sea Water (a river or lake gives ordinary Water · standing exactly between sea and fresh water gives fresh water)
    2. Make Salt from 3 Sea Water at Cooking Fire 15 (unlocked by the Salt, Sugar skill at Cooking level 25)
    3. Use the salt in Pickled Ingredients / Pickled & Spicy

    Sea Water can't be used to water crops.

    #Processing chances

    Each item can be processed 3 times. Recipes whose craft window shows Processing Chance Consumed 1 (all cooking/preservation recipes and most other processing recipes) use 1 chance from the item in the main slot.

    • At 0 the item can't be processed further — the server refuses and your ingredients are not taken (both for your own crafting and entrusted crafting)
    • Dye recipes don't use processing chances
    • Some foods made from an ingredient — Meat Dumpling, Aged Cheese, Boiled Meat — inherit the main ingredient's remaining chances minus 1
    • Old items that had been processed more than 3 times before this rule now have 0 left

    #Tips

    💡 Tip

    Preserve early — the fresher the food when you preserve it, the longer it lasts. Standard Canned Food lasts longest (6 days).

    💡 Tip

    Preservation strips the ingredient's buffs — eat food with good buff properties fresh, and preserve plain meat for storage.

    💡 Tip

    Plan each item's processing: grill once + preserve once still leaves 1 chance.
