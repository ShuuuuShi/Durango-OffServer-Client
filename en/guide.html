    Start-to-finish guide ✓ Live

    Your character's path step by step, from the first island to end-game content — what to do in each stage, with links to the detailed pages

    This page puts things in a sensible order, from creating a character to end-game content · Numbers and button steps are on each system's page (links at the end of each stage) · You can skip ahead — nothing forces this order

    #Before you play

    • Pick a server in-game, then link Discord to get a transfer key so you never lose your character — Account & Login
    • The server is still in testing — see what isn't finished on Roadmap to launch

    #Stage 1 — Day one: Ancora → Safe House

    1. Create a character and pick a former career (you get starting skills and items for it)
    2. On Ancora, follow the tasks in order: scan → 4 logs → 6 stalks → build the raft and depart
    3. Land at the Safe House, talk to K, do the missions from K (firewood · stone knife) and the New Pioneer tasks that pay T-Stones

    Read more: Getting Started · Quests and Rewards

    💡 Tip

    The early tasks pay enough T-Stones for your first essentials — don't spend it all at once

    #Stage 2 — Staying alive

    • Watch the Life · Health · Energy · Fatigue gauges — when you're tired, rest near a bonfire or under a roof
    • Drink water, wash, and eat (you can't spam food — there is hidden fullness)
    • Mind your body temperature on hot/cold islands
    • When you die, half of your bag drops at the death spot — go back for it quickly

    Read more: Survival · Eating and drinking · Buffs and status effects · Death & Revival

    #Stage 3 — Gathering and your first tools

    • Gather wood, stone and plants by hand first, then craft an axe, hammer and knife for things that need tools
    • Your tool's level must be at least the island's level to gather there — upgrade tools for the islands you visit
    • Gathered items can carry latent/rare attributes that pass on to what you craft

    Read more: Gathering · Crafting · Weapons and tools · Materials · Item attributes

    #Stage 4 — Your first home

    1. At the Safe House, take the balloon to the Tamed Island — you pick the island layout once · the Tamed Island is one shared world, you can see your friends' homes
    2. Declare a Domain of your own, then expand it — things inside your Domain decay much more slowly than outside
    3. Build a house, storage boxes, workbenches and a bonfire
    4. Give friends permissions to use your things if you want

    Read more: Islands & Travel · Domain · Building · Storage · Decay and repair

    ⚠️ Caution

    Structures outside a Domain disappear quickly — keep valuables inside your own Domain

    #Stage 5 — Long-term food

    • Make farm plots, sow seeds, water, fertilize, harvest
    • Cook at a bonfire/kitchen for buffs and taste
    • Fresh food spoils — smoke, dry, pickle or can it to keep it

    Read more: Farming · Cooking · Preserving food · Food, drinks and medicine

    #Stage 6 — Hunting and taming

    • Learn to hit with the right moves and directions, butcher carcasses for meat, hide and bone
    • Bring an animal to low health or stun it, capture it with a taming herb → train it in a pen → form a bond
    • Pets fight with you, carry you and your cargo, and produce milk

    Read more: Combat · Wild animals · Taming · Pets

    #Stage 7 — Growing stronger

    • Every action gives character EXP and skill-category EXP · spend Skill Points on new skills, rewind a limited number per day
    • Swap gear as you level, repair it when worn, dye it
    • Wear a title earned from the Career Guide, organizations and levels

    Read more: Skills and Careers · Skill list · Character stats · Equipment and repair · Clothing and accessories · Titles

    #Stage 8 — Exploring harder islands

    • Sail from a harbor to Unstable Islands of each climate — higher-level islands have stronger animals and tire you faster
    • The Unstable Index makes islands harsher — you need enough Climate Resistance before you go
    • Explore the map for warp holes, craters and rifts to earn T-Stones · unlock the quick-warp buttons

    Read more: Islands & Travel · Weather & Unstable Index · Map and exploration · Warps & Rifts

    #Stage 9 — Organizations, routines and the economy

    • Do missions and support requests for organizations to raise Trust and earn T-Stones and titles
    • Do Weekly Tasks for pioneer points, and check in daily for attendance rewards
    • Trade with players on the market, send items home through a Courier Warp Hole, collect things from your mailbox

    Read more: Organizations · Quests and Rewards · Market and Shop · Transport and Vehicles · Mail · Traps and wells

    #Stage 10 — End game

    • Team up in a clan to share land and boxes, and form alliances
    • Take on Rifts and the Warp Accelerator with friends for tiered rewards (there is a weekly cap)
    • Reach the high-Index islands, collect gear with rare attributes, build a full set of pets
    • Follow upcoming systems on Roadmap to launch

    Read more: Clans · Warps & Rifts · Items
