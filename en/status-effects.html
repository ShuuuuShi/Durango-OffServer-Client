    Buffs and status effects ✓ Live

    Every status this server can actually give players (77 of them) — numeric effects, durations and where they come from

    The status icons above your gauges are the buffs (green) and ailments (red) you currently have — touch one to read its description and time left This page lists the 77 statuses this server can actually give players (the game data has 374; the rest have no way to be obtained yet)

    How to read the Effect column

    • Fatigue from … = multiplier on climate fatigue of that kind (−50% = you tire half as fast) (Survival)
    • … /s = that gauge changes per second while the status is on · level = the status level (e.g. higher-level food gives a higher level)
    • Other values add to your stats (Character stats) · an empty Duration = it lasts while its condition holds (sitting, being in the house)
    • Most statuses are cleared when you die
