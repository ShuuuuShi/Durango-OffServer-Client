    Items ✓ Live

    Items come in categories — weapons/tools · clothing · materials · food/medicine · seeds — levels, where they come from, locking and discarding

    Every item in your bag has a category, a level, and may carry attributes. This page is the overview; each category has its own page with the real in-game icons.

    #Item categories

    CategoryWhat's in itRead more
    Weapons and toolsAxes, hammers, swords, lances, bows, crossbows, pickaxes, shovels, saws, harpoons, kitchen tools, repair kitsWeapons and tools
    Clothing and accessoriesCareer outfits, hats, gloves, shoes, bags, fashion itemsClothing and accessories
    MaterialsPlants, wood, animal parts, stone, ore, metal, processed materials, seeds, fertilizerMaterials
    Food, drinks and medicineCooked food, ingredients, drinks, medicine, dyesFood, drinks and medicine
    Structures and furnitureBuilt from blueprints, then packaged into an item you can carry and place againBuilding
    ReinsFrom capturing wild animals; tame them into petsTaming

    #Item level

    Obtained byLevel you get
    Gathering / butcheringThe source's level (plus skill bonus), capped at your character level
    CraftingAverage level of all materials, rounded down, capped at the recipe's max level
    Shop / rewards / suppliesThe level set in the pack (mostly 1)

    Each item type has a max level — the tables on the category pages show that number.

    #Where items come from

    • Gathering in the wild — plants, wood, stone, ore, soil, water (Gathering)
    • Butchering carcasses — meat, hide, bone, fat, horns, teeth (Wild animals)
    • Crafting — unlock recipes through skills and make them yourself (Crafting · Cooking)
    • Farming — seeds in farm plots (Farming)
    • Harvestable structures — wells, fish traps, planted trees (Traps and wells)
    • Shop — packs, random boxes and single items for T-Stones (Market and Shop)
    • Player market — buy from other players (Market and Shop)
    • Rewards — faction supplies and missions (Organizations) · quests, daily attendance, weekly blueprints (Quests and Rewards) · the Warp Accelerator (Warps & Rifts) · Mail

    #Item info, locking and discarding

    • Touch an item → Item Info to see its level, Durability and attributes
    • Lock items you don't want to lose — locked or equipped items can't be discarded and can't be listed on the market
    • Weapons, tools, clothing and accessories wear out with use — they can be repaired (Equipment and repair)
    • Your bag holds 200 items · you can discard items onto the ground as a bundle (Storage)
