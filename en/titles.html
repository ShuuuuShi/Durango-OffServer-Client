    Titles ✓ Live

    Earn titles from the Career Guide, organizations and character level. Wear one at a time, others can see it, and it gives stat bonuses

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    A title is shown above your character's name, and nearby players see it too. You can wear one title at a time, and the one you wear gives stat bonuses. Titles you earn are kept forever, so you can switch between them whenever you like.

    #What you can do

    • Collect titles from the Career Guide, friendship with organizations and character level
    • Choose a title to wear, or take it off
    • Use a title's bonus to support your playstyle, for example max health or crafting ability

    #How to get titles ✓ Live

    SourceCountCondition
    Career Guide58Finish a course and claim its reward (1 course = 1 title)
    The 4 main organizations16Organization friendship reaches Lv.2 / 5 / 8 / 11

    • When you earn a title during play (level-up or friendship level-up) you see Title Acquired: …
    • Titles are also granted retroactively. If your character already meets a condition, the title is in your list when you log in

    #Wearing and showing a title ✓ Live

    1. Open Character Info
    2. Press Select Title and pick one of your titles
    3. To take it off, pick No Title
    • The title appears above your head right away, and nearby players see the change right away
    • It stays after you log out and back in
    • You can only pick titles you have earned
    • The same title is used for every equipment preset

    #Title bonuses ✓ Live

    Only the title you are wearing gives a bonus. Changing or removing it updates the bonus right away. You can check the numbers on your character's Detailed Stats page.

    The 8 base stats: Strength · Charisma · Dexterity · Agility · Endurance · Will · Intelligence · Perception

    • Endurance +1 = max health +2
    • Will +1 = max energy +0.8

    Special bonuses (some titles):

    BonusEffect
    Max healthRaises your real health cap
    Max energyRaises your real energy cap
    Health regenerationRestores health every second
    AccuracyRaises accuracy on the stats page
    Crafting ability of a lineRaises that value on the stats page

    💡 Tip

    Difficulty-1 Career Guide titles are the easiest to get (17–31 Skill Points), and many of them add max health. They are a good pick early in the game.

    ℹ️ Note

    Crafting ability from a title shows on the stats page, but it does not count toward unlocking crafting mastery rewards, the same as bonuses from equipment.

    #Not finished yet ⏳ Not ready

    • The Radio University and The Lifeline titles (8) cannot be earned yet, because there is no way to gain friendship with these two organizations. The 4th Radio University title also needs a friendship level above that organization's maximum.
    • 3 special bonuses have no effect yet: random tag chance (5 titles) · defense cooldown reduction and defense stamina reduction (1 title). These titles still give their base stats.
    • Base stats other than Endurance and Will currently only show on the stats page and have no other effect.
    • The game can remember a different title per equipment preset, but the server currently uses one title for all presets.
