    Quests and Rewards ✓ Live

    The guided quest line from Ancora to the Unstable Islands, story quests, K's quests, weekly tasks and Achievements (with Skill Points), Daily Tasks, the epic story, pioneer points, Season Pass, event plans, the Halloween event, daily Attendance Rewards and the welcome mail

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    All quests live in the Task menu, with one tab per kind of quest. The server counts your progress by itself when you really do the thing: gather, craft, build, hunt, tame or reach an island. Tabs open up as you progress, and the next quest to do is shown on screen one step at a time. Rewards include EXP, T-Stones and items.

    #What you can do

    • Do the tutorial quests on Ancora, then follow the guided quest line on screen from the safehouse to the Unstable Islands
    • Follow the story quests as your character level and island level go up
    • Collect weekly task score to claim the score rewards, and earn 1 Skill Point the first time you claim each task
    • Do Achievements, Daily Tasks, Domain Construction and Animal Friend Tasks for EXP, T-Stones and Skill Points
    • Follow the epic story in the Story menu, where K and Agent D383 come to your personal Tamed Island
    • Hand in materials to raise your pioneer grade
    • Collect Season Pass points from daily/weekly missions to claim tiered rewards
    • Claim Attendance Rewards every day, and event plans while an event is on (Halloween: Oct 20 – Nov 10)

    #Weather Resistance ✓ Live

    These quests are one-time and never reset. Raise one of your 6 body resistances (heat, cold, strong sun, humidity, strong wind, volcanic heat) to Lv.5 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 50. Rewards go from 500 EXP + 100 T-Stones (Lv.5) to 3,000 EXP + 600 T-Stones (Lv.50). Read more about resistance on Weather.

    #Warp Accelerator quests ✓ Live

    26 quests about Rifts: quests for accelerator phases (1 / 3 / 6 / 10), quests for your total number of clears, and quests for each of 8 biomes. Rewards are EXP and T-Stones. See Warp. Swamp islands have 2 Rifts each, so the swamp Rift quests can be done too and every biome is covered.

    #Pioneer points and grade ✓ Live

    Hand in materials at your own Personal Communication Center to earn pioneer points. Only pioneering materials and raw food items count.

    • Points per item = 10 × 1.501^(item level − 1), so high-level items are worth many times more
    • The server caps the item level you can hand in by grade (grade 1 → level 1 · 2–3 → 2 · 4 → 3 · 5–6 → 4 · 7–8 → 5 · 9–10 → 6 · 11–12 → 7 · 13–15 → 8); above the cap the whole request is refused and nothing is taken · but Clam Meat, the only pioneering material, always counts as level 1, so it can be handed in at any level on any grade
    • Each day the first block of points (300 at grade 1, growing with grade) counts ×2, the next block of the same size counts ×1, and the rest counts ×0.1. The day resets at 07:00 Thai time
    • There are 15 grades. Your points start from zero again after each grade-up

    #Season Pass ✓ Live

    The Season Pass menu (in the main menu, next to the Shop) lets you collect season points from daily/weekly missions and claim tiered rewards — another source of rewards besides Weekly Tasks. It needs a game version that has this menu (3.1.2 or later).

    • Season 1 «Swamp Pioneers» runs from Oct 1, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026 05:00 (Thai time) · when a season ends, rewards you didn't claim are sent to your mail
    • 15 tiers · tier 1 can be claimed right away, then every 2,000 points unlocks the next tier (tier 15 at 28,000 points) · each tier has 2 slots: a Supply Box (top row) and a Special item (bottom row) · claim one tier at a time or «Claim all» · claiming opens a reward-box popup
    • Every reward item is level 60, and none of them boosts EXP

    #Halloween event ⏳ Not ready

    The Halloween event starts and ends by itself every year, Oct 20 00:00 – Nov 10 23:59 (Thai time), with an on-screen notice at the start and the end.

    • Check-in: every check-in during the event gives 3 Peepkin Seeds + 1 Spider Web Tree Seed (any slot) · odd slots 1 · 3 · 5 … 27 also give 1 event item each, in this order of 9: Halloween Floor Lamp → Halloween Streetlamp → Halloween Costume Pattern (recipe book) → Halloween Hat Pattern (recipe book) → Spider Web Tree → Comfy Halloween Bed → Halloween Hospital Bed → Halloween Curtain → Makeshift Halloween Curtain · if you check in every day you get all 9 before the event ends, even though the calendar resets at the start of November
    • Recipes and costume: your first check-in during the event (any slot), or your first claim of weekly score step 200, unlocks 10 recipes for good: Halloween Candy · Pumpkin Porridge · Pumpkin Cake · Witch Juice · Witch Jelly · Spider Skewer · Witch Sausage · Spider Cookie · Pumpkin Hat · Spooky Skull Hammer · using the costume/hat pattern books unlocks the Spooky Skull Costume and Hat recipes for good
    • Planting: both seeds can only be planted during the event, on large plots (2×2), and need no skill · the crops you harvest can still be used after the event ends
    • Shop: 6 Halloween items are sold only during the event; outside it they are hidden and can't be bought (Shop)
    • Camps: the Communication Center, bulletin board and storage of camps on every biome turn pumpkin-themed for the whole event
    • No EXP comes from this event (the quests and the Halloween food give none)

    #Welcome mail ✓ Live

    The first character of an account to enter the game gets a welcome mail with 300 T-Stones. Other characters on the same account (up to 3 per account) get a text-only copy. Update mails with attachments work the same way: the attachments are given once per account, to whichever character logs in first. Read more on Mail.

    💡 Tip

    To get the welcome T-Stones and update-mail attachments on your main character, log in with that character first.
