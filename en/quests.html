    Quests and Rewards ✓ Live

    The guided quest line from Ancora to the Unstable Islands, story quests, K's quests, weekly tasks, pioneer points, event plans, daily Attendance Rewards and the welcome mail

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    All quests live in the Task menu, with one tab per kind of quest. The server counts your progress by itself when you really do the thing: gather, craft, build, hunt, tame or reach an island. Tabs open up as you progress, and the next quest to do is shown on screen one step at a time. Rewards include EXP, T-Stones and items.

    #What you can do

    • Do the tutorial quests on Ancora, then follow the guided quest line on screen from the safehouse to the Unstable Islands
    • Follow the story quests as your character level and island level go up
    • Collect weekly task score to claim the score rewards
    • Hand in materials to raise your pioneer grade
    • Claim Attendance Rewards every day, and event plans while an event is on

    • While you are on Ancora you only see the "Ancora" tab. The other tabs appear during play, with no need to log in again (if the Task window is already open, close and reopen it)
    • The quest you are working on shows on screen, 1 per tab. After travelling or logging in again the on-screen list comes back by itself
    • Guided quests finish on their own when you reach the goal. Weekly, resistance and Warp Accelerator quests need you to claim the reward in the menu. K's quests must be handed in to K
    • Progress you made before a tab opened is not lost
    • Characters that left Ancora before this system came in still see every tab, and steps whose conditions they already meet are finished for them at login (normal reward, once)

    ℹ️ Note

    The tab names and the quests written for this server show in Thai in the game for now. The English names on this page are translations to help you find them.

    #Tutorial on Ancora ✓ Live

    New characters start on Ancora. The quests teach you to scan, collect materials, and build a raft to leave the island.

    QuestGoalReward

    Logs for the raftHave 4 logs40 EXP

    • If you left Ancora with these quests still open, they finish for you with the normal reward when you log in on another island or reach your personal island

    #Story quests ✓ Live

    There are 2 story lines. Each quest finishes on its own when you meet its condition.

    • Main line, 12 quests. It starts when you reach the safehouse (New employee, 200 T-Stones), includes steps 6 and 9 of the guided line, then continues with steps 12–20 by character level (16 · 21 · 26 · 31 · 36 · 56 · 60) and island level (40 · 60)
    • "August 33" line, 7 quests. It starts after the balloon brings you to your personal island (the "August 33" dialogue is part of the Small steps quest), then follows the highest island level you have visited (15 · 20 · 30 · 40 · 50 · 55 · 60). This line does not show on screen
    ExampleConditionReward

    Small stepsReach your personal island by balloon300 T-Stones + 3 Tropical Fruit

    • The August 33 line now gives smaller rewards than before (about a quarter of the old EXP/T-Stones). Rewards already received are not taken back

    💡 Tip

    "Radio University freshman" finishes as soon as you reach level 21. You do not need to learn anything in the Career Guide first, even though the quest text suggests it.

    #Weekly Tasks ✓ Live

    Weekly Tasks reset every Monday at 05:00 Thai time (UTC+7). All 64 tasks are shown every week; there is no random pick. If you are online across the reset, the new week's list appears by itself the next time you do something that counts for quests.

    #Weather Resistance ✓ Live

    These quests are one-time and never reset. Raise one of your 6 body resistances (heat, cold, strong sun, humidity, strong wind, volcanic heat) to Lv.5 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 50. Rewards go from 500 EXP + 100 T-Stones (Lv.5) to 3,000 EXP + 600 T-Stones (Lv.50). Read more about resistance on Weather.

    #Warp Accelerator quests ✓ Live

    26 quests about Rifts: quests for accelerator phases (1 / 3 / 6 / 10), quests for your total number of clears, and quests for each of 8 biomes. Rewards are EXP and T-Stones. See Warp. Swamp islands have 2 Rifts each, so the swamp Rift quests can be done too and every biome is covered.

    #Pioneer points and grade ✓ Live

    Hand in materials at your own Personal Communication Center to earn pioneer points. Only pioneering materials and raw food items count.

    • Points per item = 10 × 1.501^(item level − 1), so high-level items are worth many times more
    • Each day the first block of points (300 at grade 1, growing with grade) counts ×2, the next block of the same size counts ×1, and the rest counts ×0.1. The day resets at 07:00 Thai time
    • There are 15 grades. Your points start from zero again after each grade-up

    • Claim the event's weekly score step to get 1 plan

    #Welcome mail ✓ Live

    The first character of an account to enter the game gets a welcome mail with 300 T-Stones. Other characters on the same account (up to 3 per account) get a text-only copy. Update mails with attachments work the same way: the attachments are given once per account, to whichever character logs in first. Read more on Mail.

    💡 Tip

    To get the welcome T-Stones and update-mail attachments on your main character, log in with that character first.
