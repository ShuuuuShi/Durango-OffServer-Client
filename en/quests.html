    Quests and Rewards ✓ Live

    Story quests, K's quests, weekly tasks, pioneer points, event plans, daily Attendance Rewards and the welcome mail

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    All quests live in the Task menu, with one tab per kind of quest. The server counts your progress by itself when you really do the thing: gather, craft, build, hunt, tame or reach an island. Rewards include EXP, T-Stones and items.

    #What you can do

    • Do the tutorial quests on Ancora, then the new-pioneer quests and K's quests
    • Follow the story quests as your character level and island level go up
    • Collect weekly task score to claim the score rewards
    • Hand in materials to raise your pioneer grade
    • Claim Attendance Rewards every day, and event plans while an event is on

    ℹ️ Note

    The tab names and the quests written for this server show in Thai in the game for now. The English names on this page are translations to help you find them.

    #Story quests ✓ Live

    There are 2 story lines. Each quest finishes on its own when you meet its condition.

    • Main line, 13 quests. It starts with meeting K and entering the safehouse, then follows character level (16 · 21 · 26 · 31 · 36 · 56 · 60) and island level (40 · 60)
    • "August 33" line, 8 quests. It starts when you reach your personal island, then follows the highest island level you have visited (15 · 20 · 30 · 40 · 50 · 55 · 60)
    ExampleConditionReward

    💡 Tip

    "Radio University freshman" finishes as soon as you reach level 21. You do not need to learn anything in the Career Guide first, even though the quest text suggests it.

    #Weather Resistance ✓ Live

    These quests are one-time and never reset. Raise one of your 6 body resistances (heat, cold, strong sun, humidity, strong wind, volcanic heat) to Lv.5 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 50. Rewards go from 500 EXP + 100 T-Stones (Lv.5) to 3,000 EXP + 600 T-Stones (Lv.50). Read more about resistance on Weather.

    #Warp Accelerator quests ✓ Live

    26 quests about Rifts: quests for accelerator phases (1 / 3 / 6 / 10), quests for your total number of clears, and quests for each of 8 biomes. Rewards are EXP and T-Stones. See Warp.

    #Pioneer points and grade ✓ Live

    Hand in materials at your own Personal Communication Center to earn pioneer points. Only pioneering materials and raw food items count.

    • Points per item = 10 × 1.501^(item level − 1), so high-level items are worth many times more
    • Each day the first block of points (300 at grade 1, growing with grade) counts ×2, the next block of the same size counts ×1, and the rest counts ×0.1. The day resets at 07:00 Thai time
    • There are 15 grades. Your points start from zero again after each grade-up

    #Event plans ✓ Live

    During an event you get event plans one at a time, in order. Once you get one it is yours forever.

    • Claim one of the listed attendance slots during the event to get 1 plan
    • Claim the event's weekly score step to get 1 plan

    #Daily Attendance Rewards ✓ Live

    • Missing a day does not cost you your place. The next day you claim the next slot

    #Welcome mail ✓ Live

    The first character of an account to enter the game gets a welcome mail with 300 T-Stones. Other characters on the same account (up to 3 per account) get a text-only copy. Update mails with attachments work the same way: the attachments are given once per account, to whichever character logs in first. Read more on Mail.

    💡 Tip

    To get the welcome T-Stones and update-mail attachments on your main character, log in with that character first.
