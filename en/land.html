    Domain ✓ Live

    Declare a Domain as your own land and expand it up to 6 plots (8 on a Tamed Island) — structures there decay slowly and others can do nothing without permission

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    A Domain is land a player declares as their own, made of plots of 4 × 4 tiles. Structures on your own Domain decay far more slowly (7 days and they never vanish — see Decay), and nobody else can build, demolish or open boxes there without permission.

    #What you can do

    • Declare a Domain on an allowed island — free
    • Expand Domain one plot at a time (costs T-Stones) · reduce unused plots · cancel the whole Domain
    • Manage Permissions — choose what other players may do on your Domain
    • Declare a Tamed Island Domain on your own personal island
    • Clans can declare a clan Enclave (Clan)
    • Visit another player's Domain with the Domain button in their player-info popup (below)

    #How to use it

    1. Go to the spot on the island, open the Domain page and press Declare on that plot
    2. Expand: pick a plot adjacent to your Domain (up/down/left/right); you must be on the same island as the Domain
    3. Reduce: pick the plot to give up — you cannot remove the last plot (cancel instead), and the Domain must not split in two
    4. Permissions: Domain Permissions page → tick what others may do

    You cannot declare or expand over another player's structure — a plot with someone else's structure on it (including an unfinished site) is refused · discarded item bundles do not block

    ⚠️ Caution

    Reducing plots or cancelling a Domain = the structures on those plots (yours and your friends') become outside-Domain structures at once — any that nobody has used for over 10 minutes vanish almost immediately · always package them first (Decay)

    #Where you can declare

    IslandCan declare?
    Village islands · Civilized Islandyes (normal Domain)
    Your own Tamed Islandyes (Tamed Island Domain)
    Safe House Island · Unstable Island · training islandsno
    Lawless Islandno (you can still place bonfires/temporary things under the outside-Domain rules)
    Another player's Tamed Island that you are visitingno

    #You must stand at the plot when declaring ⏳ Not ready

    When you declare, you must stand close to the plot — within 4 tiles of its edge — and the plot must be on the island, otherwise the game refuses («You must stand at the plot you want to declare» / «This plot is outside the island», Thai in game) · Expanding has no distance check, you only need to be on the same island as the Domain

    Expansion cost for a player's normal Domain:

    From → toT-Stones
    1 → 2 plots2,500
    2 → 3 plots2,500
    3 → 4 plots5,000
    4 → 5 plots7,500
    5 → 6 plots12,500

    After reducing, expanding back is free up to the largest size you ever reached.

    Clan Enclave: declaring and expanding are paid from the clan fund (not the T-Stones of whoever presses the button) — expansion per plot 10,000 · 11,000 · 12,000 · 13,000 · 14,000 · 15,000 · 17,000 (size 1→2 … 7→8) · the clan must be level 5+, and the total number of Enclaves is capped by clan level — see Clan

    💡 Tip

    Expanding on a Tamed Island is completely free — if you want lots of room without spending T-Stones, build on your own Tamed Island (Islands) · structures and furniture on a Tamed Island never decay (Decay)

    #Permissions for others

    The owner can always do everything · others can only do what the owner allows:

    PermissionEffect
    Enteruse gates on the Domain (does not stop anyone walking in)
    Useuse structures — open storage/pens, craft, mannequins
    Giveput items into storage · put reins/animals into pens · add incense
    Taketake items/animals out · collect from wells/fish traps
    Buildbuild structures on the Domain
    Demolishdemolish structures

    Boxes and workbenches need several permissions at once — see Boxes, workbenches and pens

    • A newly declared Domain has every permission off — the owner turns them on in the permissions page · older Domains keep what they had
    • You can also set permissions per item (Furniture Rights) — this opens that one item even when the Domain permissions are off · you can set a daily limit on how many items others may take (a Friend slot left without a number = friends can't take anything)
    • Structures others build on your Domain (friends, or anyone you gave «Build») follow the in-Domain decay rule — 7 days and they never vanish, even if you remove the permission later · if you don't want them, demolish or package them yourself (below)

    ⚠️ Caution

    A normal box placed outside any Domain can be opened and emptied by anyone — keep valuables on your own Domain, or use a Personal Box, which only its owner can open (Storage)

    #Permissions for friends ✓ Live

    The Domain Permissions page has Friend and Close Friend tabs, separate from «others» — tick the same 6 permissions from the table above for each group.

    • Applies to player Domains and Tamed Island Domains · Clan Enclaves still use rank permissions as before
    • Which group a friend belongs to comes from what the Domain owner set in their friend list (Friend / Close Friend)
    • Friends always use the Friends tab instead of the «others» permissions — if you opened permissions for others but ticked nothing on the Friends tab, your friends can do nothing
    • The Friends tabs start with every permission off, for new and existing Domains alike — tick them yourself if you want friends to have access
    • Per-item permissions (Furniture Rights) also have Friend / Close Friend slots

    #Farm plots and gates on a Domain ✓ Live

    • On your Domain, others can sow, water, fertilize or uproot/harvest a farm plot only if they have all 3 permissions Use + Give + Take (or you opened that plot's own per-item permission) — without them the plot's buttons can't be pressed · whoever built the plot can always use it (Farming)
    • Others can open/close gates on your Domain only with the «Enter» permission · walking onto the Domain is never blocked
    • A sprinkler on someone else's Domain needs the «Use» permission

    #Boxes, workbenches and pens on someone else's Domain ✓ Live

    Structures that hold items or animals (boxes, cabinets, baskets, Personal Storage, workbenches, kilns, bonfires, drying racks, pens, mannequins, wells, fish traps, censers) standing on someone else's Domain can only be used by the Domain owner, the player who built that item, and players who have the permission matching what they want to do — without it the button can't be pressed

    StructurePermission needed
    Boxes, cabinets, baskets — open, store, take, sort, discard from the boxUse + Take, both, for everything
    Workbenches, kilns, bonfires, drying racks — craft, start/collect/cancel jobsUse + Give + Take, all three
    Personal Storage · pens · censersper button: open/view = Use · put in = Give · take out = Take
    Wells · fish traps (collecting)Take
    Farm plots · gatessee above
    • Outside any Domain nothing changed: normal boxes, pens, wells and crafting at workbenches are open to anyone · Personal Boxes, Personal Storage and unattended crafting on a workbench are owner-only
    • The player who built the item can always use it, even on someone else's Domain
    • Your animals kept in someone else's pen — you can always take them back, feed them and give them tasks
    • Reins left in someone else's taming pen on someone else's Domain: if the Domain owner removes your «Take» permission you can't get them back yourself — ask the Domain owner to take them out for you

    #Clan Enclave

    The clan leader (or a rank with the right) can declare the clan's Enclave on village/Civilized islands once the clan is level 5 or higher · declaring and expanding are paid from the clan fund (numbers above) · members get permissions by rank · non-members use the «others» permissions like a player Domain · members' structures on the Enclave follow the in-Domain decay rule. See Clan for details.

    #No wild animals spawn on your Domain ✓ Live

    Wild animals do not spawn or settle on player Domains, clan Enclaves and Domains on a Tamed Island, nor within 12 tiles around their border.

    • Animals whose home was already inside the zone move out on their own · animals chasing you can still follow you in, so stay alert
    • Fixed-spot animals, rift guards and quest animals still appear where they did before
    • Newly declared or expanded Domains take effect within a few seconds

    See Animals for details.

    #Visiting another player's Domain ✓ Live

    Tap another player → open the player-info popup → press the Domain button to warp into the middle of their Domain on a stable (Civilized) island — no sailing needed (Warp holes and rifts).

    • Anyone can use it, it is free, and the owner does not need to be online · if they have no land on a stable island the game tells you so
    • Works once you have left the Safe House (it can't be used from the tutorial island or the Safe House)
    • The destination island's normal travel rules apply
    • What you can open, use or build there depends on the permissions the owner set
    • Clan Enclaves and clan warpoints can't be visited yet · another player's Tamed Island can only be visited through the Tamed Island visit button (a Supporter's private Tamed Island that the owner has opened)

    #Safe House Island (camp island)

    • You cannot declare a Domain here
    • Player structures on this island follow the outside-Domain rule: they break within 1 day and vanish if nobody uses them for 10 minutes (Decay)
    • Objects placed by the game itself (the village, camp workbenches) are not affected
    • K's Domain (near the balloon above the camp): walking in shows the sign «K's Domain» · you can't declare over it, build, place or demolish there · K's balloon and motorbike work normally inside

    #Domain owners and other players' structures ✓ Live

    A Domain owner (on a clan Enclave: a rank that can manage the Enclave) who touches a structure someone else built on their Domain gets:

    • Demolish — materials go to the demolisher's bag · the contents are mailed back to the builder
    • Package (packageable items · free) — the package goes into the Domain owner's bag and becomes the Domain owner's (wherever you place it, it is yours) · the builder loses that structure
      • Contents (box/storage items, wall decorations, workbench jobs, incoming cargo, reins in taming pens) do not go into the package — they are mailed back to the builder · animals in pens go back to their owners · crops in farm plots are lost (as with demolishing)
      • A custom name and the text/drawing on a sign are cleared · incense in a censer stays · a house keeps its Basic Wooden Door Frame
      • Packaging a house: furniture you built yourself goes back to your bag · other players' furniture is mailed back whole to whoever built it (as a package if it can be packaged, otherwise as materials by Durability + its contents)
    • The builder always gets a notice by mail, even with nothing inside (the mail is in Thai · structure names in it are the Korean in-game names)
    • Structures with zero Durability (repair first) or not yet finished can't be packaged — they can be demolished
    • Packaging (moving) a structure is free on your own Domain/your clan's Enclave · elsewhere 100 × level T-Stones (Building)

    ⚠️ Caution

    Building on someone else's Domain = its owner can demolish or package your structure at any time (the contents are mailed back to you) — keep important things on your own Domain

    #Domain duration and upkeep ⏳ Not ready

    In the original game a Domain expired and had to be extended — this server does not have that yet: Domains never expire and have no daily maintenance fee; they last until the owner cancels them.
