    Equipment and repair ✓ Live

    3 swappable equipment sets + Decorative Gear (costumes) · weapons/gear wear out with use · repair with repair kits (4 random results) or T-Stones (always flawless) · dyeing and bleaching

    Weapons, tools, clothing and accessories are equipped to use their values (Character stats) · they wear out with use · at 0 Durability they give nothing until you repair them

    #Equipment slots

    SlotWhat goes in
    Main hand / off handOne-handed weapons, tools · two-handed items take both slots
    HeadHats · hooded outfits take head and body
    BodyTops/outfits
    Gloves · shoes · bagBy type
    AccessoryNecklaces, rings and so on
    • Equipping something that clashes with a slot takes the old item off automatically · there is no minimum level to equip — an item's values come from that item's level
    • 3 equipment sets — swap instantly (e.g. a fighting set / gathering set / building set) · all sets are unlocked
    • Decorative Gear (costumes) — some fashion items always go into a separate set (they change your look only — they don't replace your fighting gear and give no stats) — see Decorative Gear
    • Items in any set (even a spare one) can't be discarded or put in a box until you take them off

    #Other item stats apply while worn ✓ Live

    Besides attack/defense, the other stats written on an item (Max Health, evasion, weather resistance, speed, bag capacity and so on) and the item's attributes really take effect, but only for items worn in the set you are using — the effect of each stat is listed in Clothing and accessories

    #Decorative Gear (costumes) ✓ Live

    Fashion items that are Decorative Gear (many have «(Decorative)» in the name) go into the head and body slots of the Decorative Gear set, which is separate from your 3 fighting sets — your character really changes look, on your own screen and for the people around you

    • A costume covers your armor only in the slot it occupies — a slot with no costume still shows the armor of the set you're using (wear just a costume hat and your body still shows your armor)
    • A hooded costume takes the head and body slots together
    • Stats, buffs and item stats come only from the fighting set you're using — a costume adds or removes nothing
    • Take the costume off and the armor in that slot shows again · usable on every island
    • 6 costumes come with an outfit motion — the item details name the motion, and while you wear the costume it is listed as a motion that comes from the outfit (every motion is still usable by everyone, as before)

    #Wear ✓ Live

    New items have 100 Durability (a flawless craft gets 110 · materials gathered, butchered or harvested flawlessly stay at 100) · they wear when:

    ItemWears when
    The weapon you holdYou land a hit — by half the damage dealt
    Every gear piece you wearYou get hit — by the damage taken
    Gathering toolsEach successful gather (a failure wears half as much)
    Hammers/saws when buildingEach build or modification, 4 at a time
    Taming toolsEvery capture attempt

    Per use ≈ 0.35 × (1% of max Durability + damage × 0.001), at least 0.2% of max Durability — e.g. a 100-Durability sword hitting for 100 damage (half counted = 50) loses ≈ 0.37 per hit (about 270 hits)

    • At 0 Durability a weapon gives no attack, gear gives no defense and its other stats stop working · the item doesn't vanish, it can be repaired
    • The «durability» attribute on materials raises max Durability (Item attributes)

    #Repairing ✓ Live

    Touch the item in your bag → Repair (no workbench needed) · 3-second bar · only weapons/tools, clothing and accessories can be repaired · items at full Durability can't be repaired

    Required repair power = item level² ÷ 100 (rounded down · minimum 5)

    Item level1–2430405060
    Required repair power59162536

    Energy used = required repair power × 0.8

    #Repairing with repair kits

    Pick repair kits of the matching type whose total power ≥ the requirement — the kits are used up

    Repair kitRepair powerRepairs
    Tool Repair Kit — Budget / Standard / Quality30 / 90 / 150Weapons, tools
    Clothing Repair Kit — Budget / Standard / Quality30 / 90 / 150Clothing, accessories

    You can craft repair kits yourself (Weapons and tools)

    There are 4 random results — the more power you put in above the requirement (up to 2×) and the more crafting skill you have (Weapon/Tool or Tailoring), the better

    Power supplied ÷ requiredFlawlessSuccessFailureBig failure
    1× · no skill8%57%23%12%
    2× · no skill20%67%6%7%
    2× · full skill bonus32%60%5%3%
    ResultDurability after repair
    FlawlessFull · max Durability unchanged
    SuccessFull · max Durability −3%
    FailureFull · max Durability −8%
    Big failureFull · max Durability −18%

    Max Durability can drop to 2 at the lowest — below that the game won't let you repair it any more Crafting skill bonus = skill tiers learned in the category ÷ 2 (1–8)

    #Repairing with T-Stones

    Choose no kits → pay T-Stones equal to the required repair power (the number on the button — repairing an item costs T-Stones, not Warp Gems) · the result is always flawless (max Durability unchanged) — a level 60 item costs 36 T-Stones per repair

    💡 Tip

    T-Stones are cheaper and never cost max Durability — keep repair kits for when you're out of T-Stones · repairing structures uses different rules (building repair kits, or Repair Now with Warp Gems)

    #Dyeing and bleaching

    At a Dye Workbench: pick an item + a color channel (each item has 1–3 dyeable channels) + a dye → 5-second bar → that channel shifts 50% toward the dye's color (halfway between the old color and the dye) · dye again to get even closer to the dye's color Bleaching (with a decolorizer) returns the channel to the item's original color · each use consumes 1 dye · dyes and decolorizers are made at the same workbench (Food, drinks and medicine)
