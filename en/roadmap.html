    #Just went live (24–25 Sep)

    • The Shop sells the Skill Reset Ticket for 9,000 T-Stones (a single item · prices are real T-Stone prices) · everything bought in the Shop now works — capsules can be placed · animals from packages can bond right away · appearance/gender cards — Market and Shop · Skills
    • Recipe books teach a recipe/blueprint permanently — Crafting
    • Wild animals — numbers by terrain · herds of 6 that respawn inside the same herd · herd migration · hungry predators hunt · level = island ±5 · always hit back when hit within reach (no more free hits from inside a safe zone) — Wild animals
    • Domains — permissions for friends · farm plots and gates check permissions · no more lag where there are many houses — Domains · Building
