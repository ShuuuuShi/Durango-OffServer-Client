    Death & Revival ✓ Live

    Life at zero = death · revive at a warp hole, your waypoint or the landing point = half the items in your bag drop where you died, recoverable for 5 minutes · Instant Revival on the spot (100 T Stone) or CPR from a friend = nothing drops

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    When your Life reaches 0 your character dies, and you choose where to revive — reviving somewhere else drops some of the items in your bag at the spot where you died. This page covers what you lose, how to get it back, and this server's spawn rules.

    #What you can do

    • Revive at a Warp Hole you have discovered · your Waypoint on this island · or the island's landing point (items drop)
    • Instant Revival on the spot where you died — 100 T Stone or a voucher · nothing drops
    • Press Request Rescue so nearby players see a marker, and let one of them give you CPR (nothing drops) with a thank-you gift for them
    • Walk back and pick up your dropped items within 5 minutes
    • Lock important items in your bag so they never drop

    #When you die ✓ Live

    • Most statuses are removed on death (Wet, Washed, food buffs, poison…)
    • Fatigue drops depending on how many times you died in a row (the count resets if more than 1 hour passed since your last death)
    Death number (within 1 hour)Fatigue removed
    130%
    220%
    310%
    4 or moreNone

    #Dropped items ✓ Live

    ItemValue
    When it dropsWhen you choose to revive at a warp hole / waypoint / landing point — the pile is at the spot where you died (not where you revive) · Instant Revival or CPR = nothing drops
    How much drops50% of the pieces in your bag (rounded down), chosen at random
    Never dropsItems you are wearing · items you have locked (padlock icon in the bag)
    Recoverable for5 minutes after you press revive
    Pick-up rangeWalk within 3 tiles of the death spot → everything comes back automatically (same durability/colour/tags)
    After 5 minutesGone for good — logging out and coming back later does not bring it back
    • The death spot is pinned on your map so you can walk back
    • Dying again before picking up: an old pile that is still within its 5 minutes joins the new pile and the 5-minute timer restarts · a pile past its 5 minutes is simply gone
    • On lawless islands the dropped items become a bundle on the ground that anyone can pick up
    • Members of a clan of level 6 or higher get a drop-protection buff: drop share × 0.2 × (1 − 0.0125 × clan level) — e.g. about 9% drops at clan level 6, about 7% at level 25. See Clan

    💡 Tip

    Lock your tools and valuables in the bag before going after big animals — locked items never drop.

    #Reviving ✓ Live

    The death screen lets you choose where to revive:

    Revive atCondition
    Warp HoleOnly warp holes on this island that you have already discovered · items drop
    WaypointThe waypoint set on this island (a tent/bed set as home, or a checkpoint) · items drop
    Landing pointAlways available (if you have no waypoint on this island you are brought here) · items drop
    Instant RevivalOn the spot where you died · uses an Instant Revival Voucher if you have one, otherwise costs 100 T Stone (not enough T Stone = you cannot, and stay dead) · nothing drops
    • After reviving your Life, Health, Stamina and Energy are full, no matter how many times you died
    • The Instant Revival button shows its price in T Stone, matching what is really charged · your T Stone on screen drops right away
    • Some islands do not allow reviving there — if you log in while stuck dead on one, you are revived at the landing point automatically

    #CPR (help from a friend) ✓ Live

    Another player can walk up and perform CPR on your body.

    1. While dead, press Request Rescue (you can pick a thank-you gift) — players nearby, including anyone who walks up later, see a rescue marker on screen · the marker disappears by itself when you revive or log out
    2. Your helper must be on the same island, within 3 tiles of your body, and play the CPR minigame to the end (there is a 3-second countdown before it starts) — standing too far, walking off before it ends, or being on another island = no revival
    3. You get a revival offer — accept it within 30 seconds
    4. You revive on the spot for free and nothing drops · the thank-you gift you set (picked from your bag, worn items excluded) goes to your helper — straight into their bag if they are online, otherwise by mail

    #Logging out on a temporary island ✓ Live

    In the original game unstable islands could disappear, so this server decides at login: if you log out or disconnect on a temporary island, your next login puts you at the landing point of your home island (where your waypoint is) if that island is still stable, otherwise at the Safe House, with a message that your previous island is gone.

    Island you logged out onNext login
    Safe House · Ancora · Tamed Island · villages/cities · Index 1 islandsSame place
    Index 2 and up islands · event islands · training island · islands that were closedHome island → otherwise Safe House
    • Your items and pets all come with you
    • Just arriving on a temporary island does not move you — only logging back in after really leaving the game does
    • Leaving an island by any means clears your checkpoint on that island

    💡 Tip

    Logging off for a long time? Do it on your home island, your Tamed Island or the Safe House — you will come back to the same spot.

    #Safe zone around the port ✓ Live

    Within 12 tiles of an island's port/spawn point no animals spawn or walk in, and predators do not follow you in — revive at the landing point and you will not be mobbed again. But if you hit an animal first, it can hit back from the edge of the zone while you are within its reach — see When an animal is hit

    #Revival after-effects ⏳ Not ready

    The original game had after-effects of reviving (dizziness lowering accuracy · reviving with less than full gauges if you die often) — this server does not apply them yet, and is set to always revive with full gauges.
