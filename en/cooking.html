    Cooking ✓ Live

    Cook ingredients at a bonfire or kitchen — removes rawness and adds heat/cold, flavor and special properties per recipe

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Recipes in the Cooking category improve the same ingredient — grilled meat is still meat, but it is no longer Raw and gains new properties depending on how it was cooked, such as Hot (helps against cold) or Boost Umami. New dishes (bread, cheese, sausage, etc.) are made through normal crafting, see Crafting.

    #What you can do

    • Grill, roast, simmer, steam, deep-fry, stir-fry, boil, brew tea, make pills and iced drinks — every recipe removes the Raw property (no more Stomach Pain)
    • Preserve food (smoking, drying, pickling, canning) — same category, see Preserving food
    • Unlock new recipes with Cooking skills (see Skills)

    #How to do it

    1. Go to a station with a Cooking Fire (bonfire, kitchen, etc.), or a Drying Rack / Medical Lab depending on the recipe, and choose Craft
    2. Pick a recipe in the Cooking category, put an edible ingredient in the main slot and hold the tool the recipe needs (Stick, Stone Plate, Pot, Frying Pan, Metal Grate, Mortar, Container, Cutting Board)
    3. Wait for the timer bar; you get the same ingredient back, cooked
    4. Each cooking uses 1 of that item's processing chances (3 per item) — see Preserving food

    #Station levels

    Each station's Cooking Fire/Countertop has a level — a recipe appears once the level reaches what the recipe needs. A station's level = the level of that building, but no higher than the cap in this table.

    #What each property does

    PropertyEffect

    ColdEating it gives Cold Food — more resistant to heat (but colder in cold places)
    Boost UmamiSavory taste +1 and energy +3 per level

    #Tips

    💡 Tip

    A great success while cooking gives +1 level to the Hot/Cold property (e.g. Skewer gives Hot 2).

    💡 Tip

    Fire 40+ recipes don't work on a plain Bonfire (cap 19) — use a Large Bonfire or a kitchen built from high-enough-level materials.

    💡 Tip

    Some recipes, such as pills and herbal tea, need a Dried ingredient — dry, smoke or pickle it first, then make the medicine.
