    Pets ✓ Live

    Summon pets to fight, ride and carry items, feed them, use their active skills, produce milk in pens, revive them and keep them from aging

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Animals you capture and tame become your pets once you form a bond. Summon them to follow you, ride them, carry items in their bag, let them help in fights with their active skill, or put them in a pen to produce milk or train for levels.

    #What you can do ✓ Live

    • Summon / return — one at a time · animals in a pen, roaming, or knocked out can't be summoned
    • Ride — rideable species only (speed depends on species, e.g. Raptor 700 · Mammoth 160)
    • Animal bag — slots depend on species, e.g. Raptor 10 · Sabertooth 30 · Ankylosaurus 150 · Triceratops 240 · Mammoth 300 · Brachiosaurus 500
    • Feed to restore vigor · rename (1–16 characters) · set free (the animal is gone forever · items in its bag go back to your bag)
    • Roaming — let up to 5 animals roam your Tamed Island (they don't age, can't produce or train · items in their bag disappear)
    • Redraw rank (species with several ranks) — you see the rolled rank first, then accept it or keep the old one

    #How many animals you can own ✓ Live

    SkillMaximum owned animals
    None8
    Animal Handling I / II / III / IV10 / 12 / 14 / 16
    • The pet window and the top bar show «count / maximum»
    • Counted: summoned animals, stored animals, animals in a pen · not counted: animals roaming your Tamed Island, and Reins you have not bonded yet
    • Once full you can't capture wild animals, bond Reins or bring a roaming animal back — a message tells you, tools don't wear and the Reins stay in your bag · set one roaming or set one free first
    • Swapping one for one (send 1 roaming, bring 1 back) always works, even when full
    • If you already own more than the limit, none of your animals disappear — you just can't add more until you are below the limit
    • The pet window's button for bringing a roaming animal back now works

    #Vigor and feeding ✓ Live

    A pet's vigor (300 max for every species) drops only while it is summoned, and is used for fighting, skills and production

    SpeciesVigor lost per secondFull to empty
    Raptor · Direwolf · Oviraptor · Ornithomimus, etc. (18 species)0.05~100 minutes
    All other species (83)0.09~55 minutes
    FoodVigor restored
    Leaf · Minnow40
    Meat50
    Nutritious Animal Feed500
    Highly Nutritious Animal Feed700
    Infinite Vigor FeedVigor stops dropping for a while
    • Only animal food can be fed (the feeding menu lists it) · a full pet can't eat normal food
    • Attack and Provoke skills need at least half of max vigor (150)

    #Health, knockout and revival ✓ Live

    • Pet health depends on species and level · pets regenerate 0.3% health per second
    • If wild animals bring it to 0 health the pet is knocked out, returns to your list automatically, and can't be summoned until revived
    • Revive from the pet screen → full health, vigor at least half

    #Lifespan and aging ✓ Live

    • A pet lives for 30 days from bonding, then shows as "Old" — its stats are halved (health, health regen, attack, defense, speed, bag, max vigor, accuracy, etc.)
    • Roaming on your Tamed Island stops the aging clock
    • Animal Anti-Aging Remedy (15/30/45/60 days) gives life time back, but never beyond the full lifespan

    #Active skills ✓ Live

    Each pet has 1 active skill (37 skills in total) · the first draw is free · redrawing needs a ticket · strength follows the skill's rank (D–S)

    TypeExamplesEffect
    AttackBite · Rush · Back Kick · Chemical WarfareThe pet hits the target you're fighting (within 6 tiles) for ×2 the pet's attack
    ProvokeProvokeWild animals within 8 tiles go after the pet instead of you
    BuffBared Fangs · Run Free · Strong Back · Healing Helper · Pack MuleTemporarily raises your pet's or your own stats
    • Skills work while the pet is summoned · each use costs 20 vigor · cooldown depends on the skill (e.g. Bite 15 s · Provoke 33 s · Strong Back 300 s)

    #Production (milk) and training in pens ✓ Live

    Put a pet in an animal pen (Makeshift Pen · Small Pen · Medium Pen) → choose the pet → Production or Training → wait for the timer, then collect items and pet EXP

    ProductionTimeVigor usedPet EXPUnlocks at pet level
    Production Lv.11 h150401
    Production Lv.22 h17011010
    Production Lv.34 h19029020
    Production Lv.48 h2701,10030
    Production Lv.510 h4603,90040
    Production Lv.612 h59015,00050
    • Milk comes from production by mammal pets such as Phenacodus, Macrauchenia, Female Megaloceros, Direwolf, Skunkodus: each round gives Milk or Animal Droppings, 50% each
    • Male Megaloceros only gives Animal Droppings · Bumblebee Phenacodus gives Honey 50% / Milk 30% / Animal Droppings 20%
    • Amount per round = (2 + pet level ÷ 10) × the production level's multiplier (1 · 5/3 · 7/3 · 10/3 · 14/3 · 6), rounded down — a Lv1 pet on Production Lv.1 gives 2
    • 9–17% chance of a bonus item (coin · worm · corn seed) · item level = pet level
    • Training Lv.1 takes 1 h and 150 vigor for 100 pet EXP (no items)
    • Production Lv.5–6 needs more than 300 vigor, so raise your pet's max vigor first

    💡 Tip

    You can feed pets inside the pen (feed button on the pen screen) — top up vigor before starting a new production round

    #Choosing a Tamed Island ✓ Live

    The first time you go to your Tamed Island you pick 1 of 5 maps (can't be changed afterwards) · every layout has wild animals to tame and no bosses — see Choosing your Tamed Island layout
