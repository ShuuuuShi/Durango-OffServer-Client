    Storage ✓ Live

    Your bag holds 200 items · Storage Boxes hold 80 · Personal Storage has tabs of 200, up to 10 tabs · items discarded on the ground last 30 minutes

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Items live in 4 places: your bag · Storage Boxes you built · Personal Storage (a tabbed warehouse) · and bundles on the ground when you discard things. How safe boxes and storage are depends on where they stand — see Domain and Decay.

    #What you can do

    • Carry up to 200 items in your bag
    • Build Storage Boxes or Personal Storage, then Store / Take Out / sort items
    • Name and organize tabs in Personal Storage
    • Discard items onto the ground as a bundle for yourself or others to pick up
    • Package a box and move it — the contents go with it (Building)

    #Bag

    ItemValue
    Maximum200 items (every item counts; a stack of 10 = 10)
    • When full you cannot receive new items (gathering, harvesting, drawing water, …) — the game says the bag is full and the extra is not received; it does not drop on the ground
    • Items you get back (demolishing a structure, cancelling a job) still enter your bag when full, so nothing is lost
    • If you already hold more than 200, nothing is taken away, but you cannot receive new items until you are below 200

    💡 Tip

    Before a long gathering trip, store what you don't need in a box — if your bag fills up on the way, whatever you gather won't go in

    #Storage Boxes

    Touch a box → Open Storage → drag items in (Store) / out (Take Out) · stand within 4 tiles

    ItemValue
    Capacity of every box type80 items

    Who can open it:

    Box stands onNormal boxPersonal Box (locked)
    Your Domainyou + people you gave permissionyou + people you gave permission
    Someone else's Domainwhatever that Domain's owner allowswhatever that Domain's owner allows
    Outside any Domaineveryonethe box owner only

    On a Domain, permissions are split into «Use» (open) · «Give» (store) · «Take» — the box owner can also set per-item permissions and a daily limit on how many items others may take (Domain)

    💡 Tip

    Build a box on your own Domain and give friends «Use» + «Give» only (not «Take») — they can drop items off for you but cannot take anything out

    ⚠️ Caution

    A broken box (Durability at zero) cannot be opened, not even by its owner, until repaired · when a box outside a Domain falls apart, its contents drop as bundles that anyone can pick up (Decay)

    #Personal Storage

    A large warehouse split into tabs you can name.

    ItemValue
    Capacity per tab200 items
    Max tabs10 (2,000 items total)
    Tab name1–24 characters, no duplicates
    • A new one comes with one tab · you can create/rename/reorder tabs · only empty tabs can be removed
    • Only the owner can manage tabs
    • Outside a Domain only the owner can use it · on a Domain it follows the «Use/Give/Take» permissions

    #Discarding items

    Select items in your bag → Discard → they become an Abandoned Object (a bundle on the ground) at the chosen spot.

    ItemValue
    Fee (from your bag)10 T-Stones per discard
    Where the bundle goeswithin 4 tiles (none chosen = under your feet)
    One bundle holds80 items (extra returns to your bag)
    Bundle lifetime30 minutes from the last time items were added
    Bundles from structures that fell apart24 hours
    • Discarding again on the same tile adds to the existing bundle and renews its time
    • Anyone can pick it up — touch the bundle → Pick Up · an empty bundle disappears
    • Equipped or locked items cannot be discarded
    • Discarding from a box/Personal Storage/pet bag drops a bundle under your feet · discarding from a ground bundle itself destroys the item
    • Ground bundles do not block building and do not block Domain declarations

    💡 Tip

    To hand something to a friend standing next to you, discard it and let them pick it up — but anyone else can grab it too. If it isn't urgent, Mail is safer
