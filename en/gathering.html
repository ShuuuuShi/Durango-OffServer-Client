    Gathering ✓ Live

    Gather plants, wood, stone and ore, and butcher carcasses — item level, success chance, required tools and regrow times

    Everything on the islands starts with gathering — branches, leaves, stones and ore, all the way to leather and meat from animal carcasses. Every gathered item has a level; the higher the level, the better the things you craft from it. Some pieces also come with latent attributes.

    #What you can gather

    • Plants — leaves, fruit, flowers, reeds, mushrooms and more; most can be picked bare-handed
    • Wood — branches and bark by hand · logs from big trees need an axe or a saw
    • Stone and ore — big stones, ore and gems need a pickaxe or a hammer
    • Dirt — bare hands or a shovel
    • Carcasses — hunt an animal, then butcher it for leather, meat and bone (Butchering skill category) — see Animals
    • Structures that produce items — such as wells or traps, harvested in rounds — see Building

    #How to gather

    1. Walk up to the resource (within 3 tiles) and tap it
    2. The menu lists what you can gather there, with the level you will actually get
    3. Pick an entry — the game picks a suitable tool for you; entries you have no usable tool for are grayed out
    4. Wait for the progress bar to fill, then see the result: Success · Flawless Success · or Failed to Gather
    • If your Energy is low, the game asks whether you want to continue
    • Some categories need a skill first — tapping them opens the skill page for the skill you need

    Tool level rule: the tool's level must be equal to or higher than the source level (natural resources = island level · carcasses = animal level). For example, ore on a level 35 island needs a level 35+ pickaxe. Anything that can be gathered bare-handed ignores this rule.

    #Level of what you get ✓ Live

    item level = source level + skill bonus, but never above your character level
    • Source level — island level for natural resources · animal level for carcasses
    • Skill bonus — based on the total learned node levels in the Gathering category (natural resources) or Butchering category (carcasses), including the free nodes every new character starts with
    • Capped at your character level — when this lowers your item, the game tells you once per login

    The gathering menu shows the level you will actually get under this rule (it can be above the island level with a bonus). Tools, time, Energy and success chance are still based on the source level.

    #Success and failure ✓ Live

    Any gathering attempt can fail. The success chance comes from your category skill (65%) + a per-item base value (35%) + a tool bonus.

    Category skill bonusNatural resourcesCarcasses
    +086%85%
    +289%87%
    +491%90%
    +694%92%
    +7 or more95%94%
    • Gathering ability from outside skills — every point of gathering ability from titles, item attributes, item modifiers, food buffs and house ambiance adds +0.5% to the skill part (skill part capped at 99%) · it counts the ability that matches what you gather: Plant Gathering Ability (plants, wood, dirt and the rest) · Mining Ability (stone, ore, gems) · Slaughter Ability (carcasses) · Disassemble Ability (modern items) · the «Gathering Ability Buff» attribute counts for all four · points from skills are already in the category bonus and don't count twice
    • Tools add 2% + 0.2% per level above the requirement (up to +8%) + tool attributes (−6% to +8%), scaled by the tool's remaining durability · the total never exceeds 99%
    • Failure — the resource is used up and Energy is spent, but you get no item and no experience · the tool wears half as much
    • Big failure — about 1.5% of all attempts (1% when your success chance is at the 99% cap) · the tool wears more than usual
    • Flawless Success — chance 3% + (success chance − 85%) × 0.25, up to 12% · +5% when the tool you gather with has «Meticulous Gatherer» (bare-handed = your worn gear counts) · the material's main attribute gets +1 level
    • Green attributes — titles with «Latent/Rare Attribute Rate» multiply the chance to be green by (1 + the title's value), for example 30% → 39% with +30% · cap 90% · the 15% chance of getting a latent attribute at all doesn't change · see Item attributes

    Example: the Gatherer Name Tag (Gathering Ability +16 for all four types) · no tool · resource at your level:

    +289% → 97%88.7% → 93.8%
    +493% → 99%91.2% → 95.2%

    Some skills reduce the time and Energy cost (time never goes below 0.5 s). Gear, food and buffs can cut more:

    • Gathering time × (1 − reduction) — the «Gathering Time Reduction» attribute gives 1% per level on every gather (butchering too) · «Plant Gathering Time Reduction» gives 0.5% per level on plants only · both together at most −90% · for example both at level 10 while picking plants = −15%
    • Fatigue from gathering × (1 − reduction) — for example the Gatherer Name Tag halves it · at most −90%

    #When things grow back

    Each entry at a resource comes back after the time below, counted from when it was first taken. If you take everything, the resource disappears and comes back after the same time. Tapping a used-up resource tells you how many seconds are left.

    Base time 180 seconds, multiplied by the season (each season lasts 7 days):

    SeasonSpringSummerAutumnWinter
    All islands (Ancora included)~138 s180 s~257 s300 s

    #Regrowth stays on the same kind of ground ✓ Live

    When something grows back or reappears on a nearby tile, it always lands on the same kind of ground as before: freshwater things (reeds, lake fish) in freshwater · sea things in the sea · land things on land in the same area — never on a cliff or across a river or cliff wall. This is true on every island.

    #More players, faster regrowth ✓ Live

    The more players online on the same island, the faster things come back (recounted every 60 seconds · existing resources don't multiply — only the regrow time speeds up).

    Players on the island0–235102030+
    Speed×1×1.17×1.5×2×3×4
    Time (summer)180 s~154 s120 s90 s60 s45 s

    #Stone and ore come back on time ✓ Live

    • Every entry on the same resource has its own timer — someone else taking a different entry from the same rock does not restart the timer you are waiting on
    • Stone and ore always come back on time

    #Fruit and map plants grow back on time ✓ Live

    • Fruit bushes, trees and plants that are part of the map always grow back on time once fully harvested (season table above) — they may reappear on a nearby tile depending on how fertile the area is
    • Self-spread trees (new trees that sprang up during play) still follow the area's quota — if the area is already full of trees, a self-spread tree that was harvested out waits until there is room before it comes back
    • Winter still slows plant regrowth, as in the season table

    #Ancora ✓ Live

    New characters' first island now follows the original game: normal density and the normal regrow times in the season table above (the old ×4 density, ×4 faster regrowth and ×3 faster resting are gone).

    • Resources were laid out again following the original ecosystem rules and the tutorial steps — date palms by the train, reeds along the stream and lakes, stones by the lake, branches and stones around the Bonfire, logs between the beach and the raft
    • Every resource can be reached on foot (only sea fish schools sit just off the beach) · none are on cliffs
    • Sitting at Ancora's Bonfire clears a full fatigue bar in 5 seconds

    #Skill experience ✓ Live

    Each successful gather gives skill-category experience (Gathering / Butchering) based on the level of the item you got compared with your category level:

    Item level vs category levelCategory experience
    Equal or higher3
    1–5 lower2
    6–15 lower1
    More than 15 lower0

    Flawless Success ×1.5 (rounded up, max 3) · failed gathers give no experience · see Skills

    #Tips

    💡 Tip

    Always carry tools at least as high-level as the island you visit — tools above the requirement also add a little success chance.

    💡 Tip

    High-level items need a high-level character too — gathering on a level 45 island with a Lv20 character only gives Lv20 items.

    💡 Tip

    A tool with the «Extra Extra» attribute has a 10% chance to give one more item. See Item attributes.

    #One timed action at a time ✓ Live

    • Actions with a progress bar (gathering, butchering, washing, drinking, drawing water, dyeing, taming, crafting and so on) run one at a time
    • Starting a new one before the current bar is full = the new one replaces it, and the old one gives nothing · the unfinished resource stays where it was, nothing is lost
    • Results arrive only when the bar completes · for gathering, the resource is taken when the bar completes — if someone else takes it first or you walk out of range, you get nothing
    • The energy/fatigue for gathering is paid when you start — it is not refunded if the action is cancelled
