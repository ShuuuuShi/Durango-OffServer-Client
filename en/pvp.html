    Lawless Islands (PvP) ✓ Live

    Five level 60 islands where players can fight each other — open from Lv56 · who can hit whom · damage · the arrival shield · what you drop when you die · plus Brawl Island, last-one-standing matches for up to 16 players (Lv50+)

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    On normal islands players can't hurt each other — your only enemies are animals. The exception is the five Lawless Islands (the client calls this area the "Savage Islands"), where players from different sides can fight. Whoever dies leaves loot for others to pick up. No law, no land claims — just high-level resources and the toughest animals in the game.

    #What you can do

    • Sail to a Lawless Island once you reach level 56
    • Attack players who aren't in your party, clan or allied clan — area moves can hit several enemies at once
    • Pick up the packages other players drop when they die
    • Gather and hunt on a level 60 island (animals Lv55–65)
    • Play last-one-standing matches on Brawl Island (level 50+) — see Brawl Island

    เกาะไร้กฎ บึง (Lawless Swamp)Tropical

    เกาะไร้กฎ ป่าอบอุ่น (Lawless Temperate Forest)Temperate

    Island names are shown in Thai on this server, even in the English client.

    • Island level 60 · Index 1 (no resistance needed) · no lifespan — they never disappear like Unstable Islands · island fatigue like a level 60 Unstable Island
    • Wild animals are level 55–65 (island level ±5, like every level 60 island) — see Animals
    • The terrain is the same as island 4 of the same climate (e.g. Volcano 4), but it's a separate island with its own resources
    • No Domains · you can place temporary things (bonfires, camps), but they decay under the outside-a-Domain rules (Decay and repair)

    #Getting there ✓ Live

    1. At a Harbor → Sail → the sea map has 2 Lawless Island areas (tropical 3 islands · temperate 2 islands)
    2. Pick an island and confirm — if you have no clan, the confirm box warns "You can be attacked on … if you don't belong to a clan."
    • You need level 56 or higher — below that you can't sail there · sailing is free
    • Log out on a Lawless Island → you come back to the same place next time (if you're below level 56, you're moved to your home island)

    #Who can hit whom ✓ Live

    • You can hit: players who are not in your party, not in your clan and not in an allied clan — no clan = anyone can hit you
    • How: tap an enemy player → Attack, or pick them as a target in combat mode — the same moves you use on animals (Combat)
    • Hits don't land on: players who still have the Protection shield · dead players · players who are sailing away or logging out · other players' pets (pets don't attack players either) · buildings — a blocked hit shows as a miss, with a system message saying why (Thai-only)
    • No safe zone from players — the 12-tile zone around the Harbor only keeps out animals
    • On every other island you still can't hit players

    #Damage ⏳ Not ready

    The attacker's attack and armor penetration are set against the target's defense, as in when animals hit you, but the defense curve, the multipliers and the critical chance are a separate PvP set — hunting, animals hitting you and pets are unchanged.

    Damage = attack × move multiplier × armor penetration multiplier (table below) × direction multiplier × critical × kind-of-move multiplier × 0.5

    The armor penetration multiplier depends on «attacker's armor penetration − target's net defense»; between the points of the table it is a straight line

    Penetration − defenseMultiplier
    −100 or lower×0.4545
    −50≈ ×1.06
    0×1.67
    +14×1.77
    +54×2.05
    +117 or higher×2.2
    • As the target's defense rises, damage falls gradually, with no sudden jump — mid-tier armor still cuts some damage, high armor plus defense skills clearly help · defense more than 100 above the attacker's penetration doesn't help any further (floor ×0.4545)
    • A move that rolls the «ignores defense» chance from a penetration skill is counted as if the target had 0 defense

    Kind-of-move multiplier (applied last, together with PvP's ×0.5, rounded once)

    Kind of moveKind multiplierWith ×0.5
    Melee (bare hands, one-handed, two-handed, spear — area moves included)×0.8×0.4
    Ranged (bow, crossbow)×0.35×0.175
    A swing with no move data (e.g. a catapult shot)×0.15×0.075
    • The ×0.5 is a test-period value — it makes up for our characters' lower HP (about 670–920 at Lv60) compared with the original game, and can be adjusted right away based on real play
    • Skill-category bonuses don't apply between players — the damage bonus from the Melee/Ranged category levels (up to +50%) and the damage reduction from the Defense category level (up to −30%) only work when hitting animals or being hit by them · so the number floating over a head equals the HP actually lost
    • Base critical chance is 4% for every move (10% against animals, 18% for heavy moves), with your character's and skills' critical bonuses added on top as before · a crit is still ×1.9
    • Hit chance and skill-based defense penetration work the same as against animals · the target can still dodge with a roll, auto-dodge and auto-block

    Examples (hit from the front, no critical, not blocked, normal attack)

    AttackerTarget defense 0Target defense ≈166Target defense ≈359
    Bare hands (attack 40 · penetration 40)3177
    Stone axe Lv39 (122.7 · penetration 164.6)1088122
    Spear Lv60 (260.7 · penetration 300)23023099
    Bow Lv60 (199.6 · penetration 300)777733

    Skill moves hit several times harder — for example a Lv60 spear skill move (move multiplier ×2.2) against mid-tier armor ≈ 505

    • Players are never stunned or knocked down by other players · green tags that trigger on crits/dodges (e.g. bleeding) don't work against players
    • Bow/crossbow shots farther than 3.75 tiles lose damage with distance on top (down to 60% at 6 tiles · the aimed shot doesn't fall off) — see Bow and crossbow range · skill moves and the roll spend Stamina just like against animals (Move stamina) · the catapult can fire at players on Lawless Islands (Catapult)
    • Area moves (flurry, sweep, stab, strike, punch combo) hit every enemy player and wild animal in the area, once each, up to 6 targets per swing (your selected target first, then nearest to farthest) · party members, clanmates, allies and pets are never hit
    • No combat/defense category exp for hitting or being hit by players · weapons and armor wear as usual

    #Arrival shield ✓ Live

    When you log in or sail to a Lawless Island you get the Protection status for 30 seconds — other players can't hit you · it ends as soon as you move, warp or interact (attack, tap, gather)

    #Dying on a Lawless Island ✓ Live

    Death and revival work like on every island (instant revive · CPR · respawn — Death & Revival); only the dropped items are different:

    • Items drop when you press respawn, in the usual share (~50% of unlocked, unequipped items · the clan anti-drop buff still helps) — instant revive or CPR from a friend = nothing drops
    • The dropped items become a package on the ground where you died that anyone can pick up, your killer included — it lasts 5 minutes
    • Untradeable items don't go into the package — they stay in your own private pile, recovered as usual
    • The victim sees the killer's name in system chat and on the timeline · the killer sees the victim's name
    • No reward or exp for kills, only what drops

    #Brawl Island (Season 2) ⏳ Not ready

    A last-one-standing match for up to 16 players from one server-wide queue — you go in bare-handed, gather and craft weapons on the island, anyone who dies is out, and the last survivor wins (the game calls it 난투섬 «Brawl Island»)

    How to enter

    • Level 50 or higher · open at all times · main menu → the banner «난투섬에 도전하라!» → press «난투섬 입장 등록» (cancel the queue with «등록 취소») — these menu texts are still in Korean in the game
    • One queue for the whole server: with 4 players a 3-minute countdown starts (more players joining doesn't reset it · dropping below 4 cancels it), then everybody departs · with 16 players it departs at once
    • You can't queue while in a party (leave it first), and you can't create, invite to or accept a party during a match · you can't sail or warp out mid-match — the only way out is the «나가기» button

    On the island

    • One island per match, from 5 maps used in turn (tropical · temperate · volcanic · snow · swamp) · every match gets its own copy of the island
    • No wild animals on Brawl Island (as on the original island) — the only enemies are other players
    • Bare-handed: you use a separate bag that starts empty · your real bag is kept safe — nothing drops or is lost, and you get it all back when you leave (items gathered on the island don't come back with you) · skill EXP and quest progress earned on the island count for your real character
    • The same fighting rules as the Lawless Islands (Who can hit whom · Damage) — clanmates and allied clans can't hit each other
    • Dying means you leave the match — no real death penalty and nothing drops, you go back where you were with your old items · the last survivor wins («Brawl Victory») · press «나가기» to return (the game exits by itself after about 25 seconds, and the server sends you back after 90)
    • Disconnected for more than 60 seconds mid-match = you forfeit · a match lasts at most 30 minutes (then it ends with no winner)
    • No money or item rewards — you get statistics (wins, matches, kills, average kills) and a rank on the Brawl Island page

    Match timeline (counted from the first player to arrive)

    TimeWhat happens
    0:00Fog — you can't enter combat mode (you can change gear and eat)
    1:00Rain — the fight starts · combat mode can't be cancelled and you can't use or inspect items
    2:20Storm warning (message «A storm is approaching»)
    2:30Storm — health drops 15 per second unless you are inside a storm shelter (4 buildings on the map · 2 on the swamp map)
    5:00Hypothermia — health drops 10 per second even inside a shelter
    • If nobody reaches the island within 90 seconds the match is cancelled · anyone who hasn't arrived before the fighting starts doesn't take part

    #Tips

    ⚠️ Caution

    Without a clan anyone can attack you, and strong moves can kill an unarmored player in a few hits — wear armor and lock your valuables in the bag before you go (locked items never drop).

    💡 Tip

    Go with friends in the same party or clan — you can't hit each other, and your area moves never hit your own side.
