    Lawless Islands (PvP) ✓ Live

    Five level 60 islands where players can fight each other — open from Lv56 · who can hit whom · damage · the arrival shield · what you drop when you die

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    On normal islands players can't hurt each other — your only enemies are animals. The exception is the five Lawless Islands (the client calls this area the "Savage Islands"), where players from different sides can fight. Whoever dies leaves loot for others to pick up. No law, no land claims — just high-level resources and the toughest animals in the game.

    #What you can do

    • Sail to a Lawless Island once you reach level 56
    • Attack players who aren't in your party, clan or allied clan — area moves can hit several enemies at once
    • Pick up the packages other players drop when they die
    • Gather and hunt on a level 60 island (animals Lv88–99)

    #The 5 Lawless Islands ✓ Live

    Name shown in gameClimate
    เกาะไร้กฎ ภูเขาไฟ (Lawless Volcano)Tropical
    เกาะไร้กฎ บึง (Lawless Swamp)Tropical
    เกาะไร้กฎ ป่าเขตร้อน (Lawless Tropical Forest)Tropical
    เกาะไร้กฎ ทุ่งหิมะ (Lawless Snowfield)Temperate
    เกาะไร้กฎ ป่าอบอุ่น (Lawless Temperate Forest)Temperate

    Island names are shown in Thai on this server, even in the English client.

    • Island level 60 · Index 1 (no resistance needed) · no lifespan — they never disappear like Unstable Islands · island fatigue like a level 60 Unstable Island
    • Wild animals are level 88–99 (like every level 60 island) — see Animals
    • The terrain is the same as island 4 of the same climate (e.g. Volcano 4), but it's a separate island with its own resources
    • No Domains · you can place temporary things (bonfires, camps), but they decay under the outside-a-Domain rules (Decay and repair)

    #Getting there ✓ Live

    1. At a Harbor → Sail → the sea map has 2 Lawless Island areas (tropical 3 islands · temperate 2 islands)
    2. Pick an island and confirm — if you have no clan, the confirm box warns "You can be attacked on … if you don't belong to a clan."
    • You need level 56 or higher — below that you can't sail there · sailing is free
    • Log out on a Lawless Island → you come back to the same place next time (if you're below level 56, you're moved to your home island)

    #Who can hit whom ✓ Live

    • You can hit: players who are not in your party, not in your clan and not in an allied clan — no clan = anyone can hit you
    • How: tap an enemy player → Attack, or pick them as a target in combat mode — the same moves you use on animals (Combat)
    • Hits don't land on: players who still have the Protection shield · dead players · players who are sailing away or logging out · other players' pets (pets don't attack players either) · buildings — a blocked hit shows as a miss, with a system message saying why (Thai-only)
    • No safe zone from players — the 12-tile zone around the Harbor only keeps out animals
    • On every other island you still can't hit players

    #Damage ✓ Live

    Uses the same formula as when animals hit you — the attacker's attack and armor penetration against the target's defense — then multiplied by the kind of move:

    Kind of moveDamage multiplier against players
    Melee (bare hands, one-handed, two-handed, spear — area moves included)×0.8
    Ranged (bow, crossbow)×0.35
    • Hit chance, critical hits and skill-based defense penetration work the same as against animals · the target can still dodge with a roll, auto-dodge and auto-block
    • Players are never stunned or knocked down by other players · green tags that trigger on crits/dodges (e.g. bleeding) don't work against players
    • Area moves (flurry, sweep, stab, strike, punch combo) hit every enemy player and wild animal in the area, once each, up to 6 targets per swing (your selected target first, then nearest to farthest) · party members, clanmates, allies and pets are never hit
    • No combat/defense category exp for hitting or being hit by players · weapons and armor wear as usual

    #Arrival shield ✓ Live

    When you log in or sail to a Lawless Island you get the Protection status for 30 seconds — other players can't hit you · it ends as soon as you move, warp or interact (attack, tap, gather)

    #Dying on a Lawless Island ✓ Live

    Death and revival work like on every island (instant revive · CPR · respawn — Death & Revival); only the dropped items are different:

    • Items drop when you press respawn, in the usual share (~50% of unlocked, unequipped items · the clan anti-drop buff still helps) — instant revive or CPR from a friend = nothing drops
    • The dropped items become a package on the ground where you died that anyone can pick up, your killer included — it lasts 5 minutes
    • Untradeable items don't go into the package — they stay in your own private pile, recovered as usual
    • The victim sees the killer's name in system chat and on the timeline · the killer sees the victim's name
    • No reward or exp for kills, only what drops

    #Tips

    ⚠️ Caution

    Without a clan anyone can attack you, and strong moves can kill an unarmored player in a few hits — wear armor and lock your valuables in the bag before you go (locked items never drop).

    💡 Tip

    Go with friends in the same party or clan — you can't hit each other, and your area moves never hit your own side.
