    Clothing and accessories ✓ Live

    Career outfits, hats, gloves, shoes, bags and fashion items — where to get them, examples with icons

    Clothing gives defense and stats (see Character stats) · equipping, removing and repairing is on Equipment and repair Early career outfits are made at a Workbench, the advanced Lv60 ones are sewn at a Tailoring Workbench · most fashion items are sold in the Shop — fashion packs (paid in Durango Coins) or random Express Cargo boxes (Fashion / Decorative Gear, 100 Warp Gems per box) · profession name tags come in 100-Durango-Coin packs · a few can be bought with Loyalty points at the Loyalty Shop

    💡 Tip

    The «Where from» column in the tables lists the easiest way first — «Craft (hands)» = anywhere, no workbench · a name in brackets = the workbench you must stand next to · Shop prices are in Durango Coins / Warp Gems / Loyalty as written · «Not obtainable yet» = there is no way to get that item on this server right now (its level shows «—»)

    #Special item stats really work ✓ Live

    The stats written on an item (besides defense) and the item's attributes really apply while you wear it — they add to the stats window and the gauges on screen

    Item statEffectExample
    Max Health · Max Energy · Life recoveryLonger gauges, faster regeneration (Survival)Ace Explorer Outfit Max Energy +10 to +15 depending on item level · some event items add Max Health
    EvasionA chance to dodge animal attacks by itself (Character stats)Melee Combat Outfit +1 to +5 depending on item level
    Heat/cold/wind … resistance3% less fatigue of that weather per point (up to 90%), and softer temperature changes (Weather)Leaf Outfit (heat) · Thermal Resistance Gloves +10 to +12 · Winter Fur Gloves Lv60 Cold Resistance 4
    Volcanic Storm ImmunityNo Precursor and no Volcanic Storm damage (Volcanic Storm)Firefighter Outfit/Helmet
    Movement speedWalk faster (Weather)Techwear / Techwear Hat +10% each
    StealthAggressive animals spot you from closer (Wild animals)Ghillie Suit · Combative Search Outfit
    Bag capacityCarry more (Storage)Bag-pocket modification/Storage Capacity attribute: level = slots · some event bags +20
    Acquired EXPMore character EXP (Skills)A special event item +200%
    Gathering fatigue reductionLess tired from gathering (Gathering)Gatherer Name Tag −50%
    • Stats that rise in steps with level use the item's level (an item below the first step gets the first step's value)
    • Attribute levels on an item (resistance, defense, capacity …) = the item's design level + its latent attribute (Item attributes)
    • An item at zero durability gives nothing at all — attributes, immunities and bag slots included — until repaired · stats update as soon as it breaks or is repaired (Equipment and repair)
    • Crafting fatigue reduction (e.g. the Builder Name Tag) has no effect yet, because crafting on this server doesn't add fatigue in the first place
