    Organizations ✓ Live

    Do missions and support requests for organizations to raise Trust, earn T-Stones, Warp Gems and titles, modify equipment with Tech Support, and run personal research

    ⏳ Some sections on this page are not ready yet

    Durango has several organizations that stay in touch with pioneers by radio. Working for an organization raises your Trust (friendship) with it. When your Trust levels up you get T-Stones, Warp Gems, titles and access to higher support-request tiers.

    #What you can do

    • Take missions at a camp Communication Center. Finishing one gives T-Stones, EXP and Trust (the first mission of the day also gives Warp Gems)
    • Use Request Support to pay T-Stones for materials or tools, and gain Trust too
    • Modify equipment, then use Tech Support to re-roll the modification
    • Run personal research in your own labs for a 30-minute buff

    #Missions ✓ Live

    Where: the Communication Center in a camp. For delivery missions, bring the items to the camp's Storage.

    • There are 84 missions — 44 regular missions (11 per organization) plus 40 high-level island missions that are only offered on Tundra/Snowfield/Tropical/Swamp islands of island level 40+ · new characters at the safehouse also get 6 separate lesson missions
    • You can hold 1 mission per organization (4 at most)
    • Goals vary: deliver, craft, build, hunt, gather, explore and tame. Some missions have several goals
    • Harder missions need a higher character level or Trust level
    • Daily reward cap: each character gets rewards for 20 missions per day (all organizations together), reset at 05:00 Thai time. After that you can still finish missions, but you get no T-Stones, EXP, Trust or first-mission bonus (the System chat tells you). They still count for the weekly Pioneer Mission tasks

    First-mission bonus: the first mission you finish gives an extra 5 Warp Gems + 100 EXP + 5 Trust to that organization (there is no bonus T-Stone payment any more). It becomes available again 22 hours after you claim it.

    Cancel: allowed any time with no point loss, but you then wait for a new mission based on your character level.

    Accept Another Mission: re-roll a mission you have not accepted yet. You have 3 charges and get 1 back every 30 minutes.

    The confirm window in the game does not show a T-Stone price; the real price appears in the System chat before you confirm. Not enough T-Stones = it fails and nothing changes.

    Special mission rules

    • Recovery test (resting): sit at a campfire or tent for a full 2 minutes, then stand up, log out or warp = 1 count. Standing up early or dying while sitting = no count
    • Claim land: not offered to characters who already own land of their own (clan land does not count)
    • Hot meals / Pioneer feast: roast any kind of meat (meat-head hammers don't count). Sturdy house / Master builder: a tent counts too
    • Explore a dangerous island / Explorer: if you accept while already standing on an Unstable Island, the "travel to the island" goal counts right away
    • Animals to hunt: accept one of The Company's hunting or butchering missions (Clear the area · Supplies for the camp · Animal hides · Bones for tools) while on the safehouse and 3 Compsognathus appear around you. This happens once per character, and killed ones do not respawn

    Mission names are server text and show in Thai in the game; the names above are translations.

    💡 Tip

    Missions on high-Index islands multiply your EXP several times, and they also count toward the weekly "Pioneer Mission" tasks. See Quests.

    #High-level island missions ✓ Live

    A new set of 40 missions from the DurangoFAQ community Mission List. They are only offered while you are on a Tundra, Snowfield, Tropical or Swamp island of island level 40 or higher (other islands keep offering the usual 44 missions).

    • You need the character level in the table (35–45) and Trust Lv.3 (130 points) or higher with that organization
    • On a matching island these missions come up more often than low-level ones
    • "Hunt … and bring …" missions have 2 goals: hunt that animal (every variant with the same name counts, e.g. White Direwolf), then deliver the part — a part from any animal works, because items in your bag don't remember which animal they came from
    • Happy New Year counts when you capture a Male Megaloceros and get its reins (not when you domesticate it in a cage)
    • No Hesitation: Coelophysis doesn't drop a skull when butchered — a skull from any other animal works
    • Rewards follow the same rules as other missions (20 per day cap · first-mission bonus · EXP × island Index multiplier)
    • Mission names and texts in game are in Thai

    #Request Support ✓ Live

    Pay T-Stones to request goods from an organization. You get the items right away, plus 1 random extra item, plus Trust.

    • There are 10 tiers. The tiers you can use match your Trust level with that organization (tier 1 works from 0 points)
    • Each organization has 3 requests per tier. Across all organizations there are 53 kinds of request
    • Item level = your character level rounded down to a multiple of 5 (minimum 1), e.g. a Lv23 character gets Lv20 items. Items with a limited level range are kept inside that range. The request list shows the same level you actually get
    • If a request levels up your Trust, you get the T-Stones and Warp Gems from the table above, the same as for missions

    • After a request, that organization rests for 30 minutes before the next one

    #Equipment modification and Tech Support ✓ Live

    Modification adds special stats to equipment (shoes, weapons, bags, gloves, hats, tools, clothes). Each item has 1 slot.

    First modification: craft a modification recipe at a workbench, using the item as an ingredient. The stats are rolled into the slot right away. There are 22 modification recipes. Some come from Tailoring and Weapon/Tool skills (Skills). Some recipes also add a penalty, such as lower max energy.

    • You must have learned the recipe. A tier-2 recipe also needs the recipe before it. The slot must be empty (a tier-2 recipe does not upgrade over tier 1)
    • Crafting EXP and quest progress count as soon as the progress bar starts, so logging out mid-bar loses nothing
    • If the materials are missing or don't fit, you get your energy back

    Tech Support: use it at a Personal Communication Center or a camp Communication Center to re-roll the slot.

    1. Request an estimate. You see the result before you commit. You can lock stats you want to keep (at least 1 stat must stay unlocked)
    2. Apply. Pay the recipe's materials again plus energy, and you get exactly what the estimate showed. The item then becomes untradable (see the box below)
    3. Reset the slot if you want to start over. It costs 50 Warp Gems

    ⚠️ Caution

    Equipment that has had Tech Support applied is untradable:

    • It cannot be listed on the market
    • Dropping it on the ground destroys it. Nobody can pick it up, not even you (dropping only this item costs no bundle fee)
    • It can only be stored in boxes, warehouses, mannequins and cargo exits you built yourself. Other players', clan, friends' or public boxes refuse it and it stays in your bag

    ℹ️ Note

    The reset-slot button shows a price of 50 with a gem icon, and 50 Warp Gems are really taken (estimates are still paid in T-Stones).

    #Personal research ✓ Live

    Build a Life Lab, Light Tech Lab or Medium Tech Lab, then pay T-Stones to research. You get a buff right away that lasts 30 minutes. For example, butchering research raises your butchering ability.

    Research tierCost (T-Stones)

    • There are 80 research options
    • Each lab type holds 1 buff at a time. Starting a new research replaces the old one
    • The lab's number sets the highest research tier it can run
